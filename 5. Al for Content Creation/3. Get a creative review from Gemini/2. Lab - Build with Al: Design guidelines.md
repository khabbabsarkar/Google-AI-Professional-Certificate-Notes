## 1. Introduction

Good design needs clear rules and planning.

Gemini can help you create design guidelines, review creative assets, and prepare feedback notes.

In this lab, you will learn how to use Gemini to review a flyer and suggest improvements based on specific design guidelines.

The lab has three main steps:

1. Get design guidelines.
2. Get specific feedback on a creative asset.
3. Draft a feedback note.

## 2. Step 1: Get Design Guidelines

First, open Gemini and start a **New chat**.

Enter a prompt to get design guidelines for your creative asset.

### Example Prompt

```text
What are some guidelines that should be followed
when designing cybersecurity awareness flyers
for a cybersecurity organization?

Include guidelines for:
- Layout and structure.
- Font style and readability.
- Color selection.
- Images and graphics.
- Visual hierarchy.
- Message clarity.
- Call to action.

Keep the guidelines practical and easy to follow.
```

Review Gemini's response.

These guidelines will help you evaluate your design in the next step.

## 3. Step 2: Get Specific Feedback on Your Asset

In the same Gemini chat:

1. Upload your creative asset (such as a flyer or poster).
2. You can use your own design or the sample flyer provided in the lab.
3. Explain the purpose of the design.
4. Tell Gemini who the target audience is.
5. Ask Gemini to review the design using the guidelines from Step 1.

### Example Prompt

```text
Review this cybersecurity awareness flyer.

The goal is to educate university students
about phishing attacks and encourage safe
online behavior.

Provide constructive feedback based on
the design guidelines above.

Requirements:
- Highlight design weaknesses.
- Identify readability problems.
- Review colors, images, and layout.
- Check whether the message is clear.
- Suggest specific improvements.
- Prioritize the most important changes.

Be honest, clear, and practical.
```

Read the feedback carefully.

Focus on the problems and suggestions that can make the biggest improvement.

## 4. Step 3: Draft a Feedback Note

After reviewing the design, ask Gemini to prepare a short feedback note.

This note can be shared with the designer or another team member.

### Example Prompt

```text
Write a short and professional feedback note
to share these recommendations with the
designer of this cybersecurity awareness flyer.

Include:
- Positive points about the design.
- Main issues that need improvement.
- Specific recommendations.
- The most important changes to make first.

Use polite, clear, and simple English.
Keep the note short and actionable.
```

## 5. Real-Life Cybersecurity Example

### Reviewing a Phishing Awareness Poster

Imagine you are preparing a poster for a university cybersecurity awareness campaign.

You want the poster to encourage students to recognize phishing messages.

You can use Gemini in three steps:

| Step | What to Do | Expected Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Guidelines | Ask Gemini for poster design rules. | A list of design standards. |
| 2. Feedback | Upload the poster and request a review. | Specific problems and improvements. |
| 3. Feedback Note | Ask Gemini to prepare a note for the designer. | A professional feedback message. |

### Possible Design Feedback

- Make the headline larger.
- Use better color contrast.
- Reduce unnecessary text.
- Highlight common phishing warning signs.
- Add a clear call to action: **Think Before You Click.**

## 7. Best Practices

1. Define the purpose of the design.
2. Understand the target audience.
3. Ask for clear design guidelines.
4. Upload a good-quality image or file.
5. Request specific and constructive feedback.
6. Prioritize important improvements.
7. Keep feedback polite and actionable.
8. Review AI suggestions before sharing them.

## 8. Key Notes

- Gemini can generate design guidelines for different creative assets.
- You can upload images and ask for detailed feedback.
- AI can identify weaknesses and suggest practical improvements.
- Gemini can turn design feedback into a professional note.
- Clear feedback helps teams improve creative work faster.

Good design comes from good decisions. AI can help identify problems, but human creativity and judgment make the final design better.
