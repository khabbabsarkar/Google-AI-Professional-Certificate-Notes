## 1. Introduction

A good design should be clear, attractive, and easy to understand.

Sometimes, we feel that a flyer or poster does not look right, but we cannot identify the exact problem.

Gemini can use its **multimodal capabilities** to review images and text together. It can analyze a design, identify problems, and suggest improvements.

In this lab, you will learn how to use Gemini to review a business flyer and create clear design feedback.

## 2. What Is Multimodal Prompting?

Multimodal prompting means giving AI different types of information, such as:

- Text
- Images
- Documents
- Audio
- Video

For example, you can upload a flyer image and give Gemini written instructions.

Gemini can review both the visual design and the text in the flyer.

## 3. Benefits of AI-Powered Design Review

| Benefit | Explanation |
|---|---|
| Specific feedback | Identifies problems in the design. |
| Better decisions | Provides clear suggestions for improvement. |
| Saves time | Reduces manual design review work. |
| Better collaboration | Makes feedback easier to explain to others. |
| Clear communication | Helps teams understand what needs to change. |

## 4. What Can Gemini Review?

Gemini can help evaluate different parts of a flyer.

| Design Element | What to Check |
|---|---|
| Layout | Are the elements arranged properly? |
| Typography | Are the fonts clear and readable? |
| Colors | Do the colors work well together? |
| Images | Are the images relevant and clear? |
| Spacing | Is there enough space between elements? |
| Hierarchy | Is the most important information easy to find? |
| Call to Action | Is the next step clear to the audience? |

## 5. Practical Gemini Prompt

Imagine you have created a flyer for a cybersecurity awareness campaign.

Upload the flyer image to Gemini and use this prompt:

```text
Act as a professional graphic designer
and visual communication expert.

Review the attached cybersecurity awareness flyer.

Evaluate these areas:
1. Layout and structure.
2. Font style and readability.
3. Color combination.
4. Image quality and relevance.
5. Spacing and alignment.
6. Visual hierarchy.
7. Overall message and clarity.

Identify specific design problems.

For each problem:
- Explain why it is a problem.
- Suggest a practical improvement.
- Explain how the change can improve the design.

Give honest and constructive feedback.
Do not make assumptions about information
that is not visible in the flyer.
```

## 6. Create a Feedback Note

After receiving the design critique, you can ask Gemini to turn the feedback into a short note.

This makes it easier to share suggestions with a designer or team.

### Example Prompt

```text
Turn the design review into a professional
feedback note.

Include:
- Main design issues.
- Suggested improvements.
- Important changes to make first.
- Positive points about the current design.

Use clear, polite, and simple English.
Keep the note short and actionable.
```

## 7. Real-Life Cybersecurity Example

### Review a Phishing Awareness Flyer

Imagine you are preparing a flyer to educate university students about phishing attacks.

You upload the flyer to Gemini for review.

Gemini may identify issues such as:

- The headline is too small.
- The background makes the text difficult to read.
- Too many words make the flyer crowded.
- The phishing example is not clear.
- The safety tips are difficult to find.
- The call to action is missing.

### Suggested Improvements

| Problem | Improvement |
|---|---|
| Small headline | Use a larger, clearer headline. |
| Poor contrast | Choose colors that make text easier to read. |
| Too much text | Use short and simple messages. |
| Weak visual hierarchy | Highlight the most important warning. |
| Missing CTA | Add a message such as "Think Before You Click." |

This helps create a more effective cybersecurity awareness flyer.

## 8. Best Practices

1. Upload a clear image of the design.
2. Explain the purpose of the flyer.
3. Identify the target audience.
4. Give Gemini clear review instructions.
5. Ask for specific problems, not just general opinions.
6. Request practical improvement suggestions.
7. Review the feedback before making changes.
8. Keep the original design file for comparison.

**Important:** AI feedback is a starting point. Human judgment is still needed to make the final design decisions.

## 9. Key Notes

- Gemini can review images and text together.
- Multimodal prompting helps AI understand visual content.
- AI can identify design problems and suggest improvements.
- Design feedback can be converted into a clear note.
- These techniques can improve collaboration and save time.

Good feedback does more than say something looks wrong. It explains what needs improvement and how to make it better.
