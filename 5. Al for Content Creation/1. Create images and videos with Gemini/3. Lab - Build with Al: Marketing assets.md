## 1. Introduction

In this lab, you will learn how to use Gemini to create custom marketing images and videos.

You will use three main steps:

1. Brainstorm visual ideas.
2. Generate a custom image.
3. Turn the image into a video.

These skills can help you create content for:
- Social media.
- Marketing campaigns.
- Presentations.
- Email illustrations.
- Personal branding.
- Business promotions.

## 2. Requirements

Before starting the lab:

- Sign in to Gemini using your Google Account.
- Open a new chat.
- Follow the video instructions.
- Customize the prompts with your own information.

**Note:** Some image and video generation features may require an eligible account or plan.

## 3. Lab Tasks

### Step 1: Generate Ideas for a Visual Marketing Asset

First, open Gemini and click **New chat** from the left-side menu.

Use the prompt below to generate different ideas.

**Prompt:**

```text
I want to create a [visual marketing asset, e.g. logo or social media image].

We offer [your company's services or products] to
[your target audience].

Give me a few creative ideas that range from
[one style, e.g. logical] to [another style, e.g. whimsical].
```

**What to do:**

1. Replace the text inside the brackets.
2. Send the prompt to Gemini.
3. Review the suggested ideas.
4. Choose your favourite concept.

You will use this concept to generate an image in the next step.

### Step 2: Generate an Image

Continue in the same Gemini chat.

Choose your favourite idea and ask Gemini to create an image.

**Prompt:**

```text
I like [your favourite option from Gemini's previous response].

Generate an image of [your visual asset] in a
[visual style, e.g. futuristic] style.

Make it [overall feeling, e.g. warm and professional]
and include [any additional requirements, such as text].
```

**What to do:**

1. Replace the bracketed text.
2. Describe the image clearly.
3. Generate the image.
4. Review the result.
5. Make changes if needed.
6. Download the final image.

You will use this image to create a video.

### Step 3: Generate a Video

Now, turn your generated image into a video.

**Instructions:**

1. Open the Gemini tools menu.
2. Select **Create videos (Veo 3.1)**.
3. A new chat will open.
4. Attach the image you generated in Step 2.
5. Paste the prompt below.
6. Replace the bracketed text with your requirements.
7. Generate and review the video.

**Prompt:**

```text
Attached is a [marketing visual asset, e.g. mascot for our company].

Create a video for [distribution channel, e.g. social media]
where [describe the subject, action, and scene].

Also include [optional visual style, audio, dialogue,
and camera movement].

Our company is called [company name] and it offers
[company services or products].
```

**Example:**

Create a short promotional video for social media using the attached cybersecurity mascot. Show the mascot appearing in a modern digital environment with animated security icons and subtle technology effects. Use smooth camera movement and a professional futuristic style.

Our brand focuses on cybersecurity education, ethical hacking, and AI security.

## 4. Cybersecurity Application

You can use the same workflow to promote a personal cybersecurity brand.

### Example: Create a Cybersecurity Awareness Campaign

| Step | Activity |
|---|---|
| 1. Brainstorm | Generate ideas for a phishing awareness poster |
| 2. Image | Create a custom phishing awareness visual |
| 3. Video | Animate the visual into a short awareness video |

Other possible marketing assets:

- Ethical hacking promotional graphics.
- Cybersecurity learning banners.
- AI security awareness videos.
- Digital forensics infographics.
- LinkedIn promotional visuals.
- Personal brand mascots.

## 5. Complete Workflow

```text
Start a New Gemini Chat
          |
          v
Brainstorm Visual Ideas
          |
          v
Choose Your Favourite Concept
          |
          v
Generate a Custom Image
          |
          v
Review and Download Image
          |
          v
Open Veo 3.1
          |
          v
Attach the Generated Image
          |
          v
Generate a Video
          |
          v
Review the Final Marketing Asset
```

## 6. Important Tips

- Be specific when describing your visual ideas.
- Clearly define your target audience.
- Choose a visual style that matches your brand.
- Keep colours and design consistent.
- Describe the movement and camera style when creating videos.
- Check generated text, logos, and visual details.
- Review the final content before publishing.

## 7. Key Notes

- Gemini can help brainstorm marketing ideas.
- You can turn text descriptions into custom images.
- Veo 3.1 can turn images into videos.
- Multimodal prompting connects text, images, and video.
- The same workflow can support marketing, presentations, and personal branding.
