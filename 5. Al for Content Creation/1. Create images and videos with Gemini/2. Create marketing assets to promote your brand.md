## 1. Introduction

AI tools like Gemini can help turn simple ideas into professional marketing assets.

You can start with a text description and create images or videos for your brand.

This process uses **multimodal prompting**, where AI works with different types of media, such as text, images, and videos.

For example:

- Text → Image
- Image → Video
- Text + Image → Video

This makes it easier to explore creative ideas and produce marketing content.

## 2. What Are Marketing Assets?

Marketing assets are visual or written materials used to promote a brand, product, or service.

Examples include:

- Brand logos.
- Mascots.
- Social media graphics.
- Promotional posters.
- Product images.
- Short marketing videos.
- Advertisements.
- Brand banners.

These assets help a brand communicate with its audience and build recognition.

## 3. What Is Multimodal Prompting?

Multimodal prompting means giving AI different types of input to create or transform content.

For example, you can:

1. Describe an idea using text.
2. Generate a custom image.
3. Use that image as input for video generation.
4. Create an animated marketing video.

### Example Workflow

| Step | Input | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Text description | Creative concept |
| 2 | Selected concept | Custom image |
| 3 | Generated image | Animated video |
| 4 | Final visual | Marketing asset |

## 4. What Will You Learn in This Lab?

The lab demonstrates how to use Gemini image and video generation models.

### Step 1: Brainstorm Concepts

Start by generating different ideas for your brand.

For example, imagine you are creating a mascot for an IT support company.

Ask Gemini to suggest different mascot concepts based on your brand identity and audience.

### Step 2: Generate Custom Images

Choose your favourite concept and create a custom image.

You can describe:
- Main subject.
- Visual style.
- Colours.
- Background.
- Lighting.
- Composition.

### Step 3: Create Animated Videos

Use your generated image as input for video generation.

Describe how you want the subject to move and what should happen in the video.

### Step 4: Review and Improve

Check the generated images and videos.

Make changes to improve their quality and match your brand identity.

## 5. Cybersecurity Example: Build a Personal Cybersecurity Brand

Imagine you want to promote your personal cybersecurity brand on LinkedIn.

You can use Gemini to create different marketing assets.

### Asset Ideas

| Asset | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Personal mascot | Build brand recognition |
| Cybersecurity poster | Share security awareness |
| LinkedIn banner | Improve profile appearance |
| Short video | Explain cybersecurity topics |
| Infographic | Present technical information |
| Promotional graphic | Highlight learning achievements |

### Example: Create a Cybersecurity Mascot

**Prompt 1: Brainstorm Ideas**

"Act as a professional brand designer. Suggest five creative mascot concepts for a personal cybersecurity brand focused on ethical hacking, digital forensics, network security, and AI security. Explain the visual style, colours, personality, and message of each concept."

**Prompt 2: Generate an Image**

"Create a professional cybersecurity mascot based on this concept. Use a modern futuristic design with dark blue and black colours. Include subtle digital security elements. Make the character friendly, intelligent, and suitable for a professional LinkedIn brand."

**Prompt 3: Turn the Image into a Video**

"Animate this cybersecurity mascot into a short professional promotional video. Make the character move naturally, add subtle digital effects, and use a modern technology-themed background. Keep the original character design consistent."

## 6. Benefits of AI-Powered Marketing Asset Creation

### 1. Exploration with Less Risk

You can test different creative ideas before spending money on professional production.

### 2. Multimodal Creativity

You can move from text to images and from images to videos.

### 3. Faster Execution

AI can help you create visual concepts and marketing materials in less time.

### 4. Better Brand Communication

You can create content that matches your brand voice, visual style, and target audience.

## 7. Important Things to Remember

- Give clear instructions to AI.
- Keep your brand colours and style consistent.
- Review generated images and videos carefully.
- Check text, logos, and visual details.
- Respect copyright and image-use permissions.
- Do not publish misleading or inaccurate content.
- Keep your own creative direction and judgment.

## 8. Key Notes

- Gemini can help create marketing images and videos.
- Multimodal prompting connects different types of media.
- You can brainstorm ideas, generate images, and animate them.
- AI makes it easier to test creative concepts before production.
- Consistent visual identity helps build brand recognition.
- Human creativity and review are still important.
