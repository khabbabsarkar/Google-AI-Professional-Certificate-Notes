## 1. Introduction

A good slide deck should communicate important information clearly and quickly.

Long text and too many details can make slides difficult to read.

Gemini in Google Slides can help improve slide text and generate custom images for important points.

In this lab, you will learn how to make presentations more readable, visual, and engaging.

## 2. Lab Requirements

Before starting:

- Sign in to Gemini using your Google Account.
- Open Google Slides.
- Use your own presentation or the sample slide deck provided in the lab.
- Customize the prompts based on your presentation topic.

## 3. Step 1: Edit Slide Text

First, choose a slide that you want to improve.

Follow these steps:

1. Open your presentation in Google Slides.
2. Select the slide you want to improve.
3. Click the **Gemini icon** in the top-right corner.
4. Open the Gemini sidebar.
5. Enter a prompt to improve the slide text.
6. Review Gemini's suggestions.
7. Replace the original text with the version you prefer.

### Example Prompt

```text
I want to make this slide easier to read.

Write three sets of short headlines and descriptions
that can replace the text on this slide.

Requirements:
- Use simple English.
- Keep the main meaning.
- Make the headlines clear and interesting.
- Keep descriptions short.
- Focus on the most important information.
```

### Why This Is Useful

- Reduces long paragraphs.
- Makes important points easier to find.
- Improves readability.
- Helps the audience understand the message quickly.

## 4. Step 2: Generate Images for Key Points

After improving the text, create images to support the main ideas.

Generate images one at a time using the Gemini sidebar.

### Example Prompt

```text
Generate an image to illustrate
a phishing attack.

Make it dark blue and white,
with a clean and modern background.

Use a professional illustration style
suitable for a cybersecurity presentation.
```

Review the generated images and insert your preferred image into the slide.

### Create More Images in the Same Style

You can ask Gemini to create another image using the style of your selected image.

Example prompt:

```text
I like the top-right image option best.

Create another illustration in the same style,
this time showing a cybersecurity analyst
monitoring suspicious network activity.

Keep the same colors and visual style.
```

Repeat this process for other key points.

## 5. Real-Life Cybersecurity Example

### Create a High-Impact Phishing Awareness Slide Deck

Imagine you are preparing a presentation about phishing attacks.

You can improve the slides using short text and custom AI-generated images.

| Slide Topic | Short Headline | Suggested Image |
|---|---|---|
| Phishing | Think Before You Click | Suspicious email |
| Fake Websites | Check Before You Trust | Fake login page |
| Social Engineering | Attackers Target People | Person receiving a suspicious message |
| Email Security | Check the Sender | Email warning illustration |
| Prevention | Stay Alert and Stay Safe | Cybersecurity protection illustration |

### Example Slide Text

**Headline:** Think Before You Click

**Description:** Check the sender, review suspicious links, and never share sensitive information through unknown websites.

**Image idea:** A person looking at a suspicious email on a computer screen.

This combination of short text and relevant images makes the slide easier to understand.

## 6. Best Practices for High-Impact Slides

1. Use short and meaningful headlines.
2. Keep descriptions simple.
3. Focus on one key idea per slide.
4. Choose images that explain the message.
5. Maintain consistent colors and image styles.
6. Avoid unnecessary text and decorations.
7. Review AI-generated images before using them.
8. Check that the images match the slide content.

## 8. Key Notes

- Gemini can improve slide text with short headlines and descriptions.
- AI can generate custom images for important points.
- You can create multiple images with a consistent visual style.
- Short text and relevant visuals make presentations more engaging.
- Human review is important for accuracy and design quality.

A high-impact presentation combines clear messages with meaningful visuals. AI can help create both, but your ideas and judgment make the presentation valuable.
