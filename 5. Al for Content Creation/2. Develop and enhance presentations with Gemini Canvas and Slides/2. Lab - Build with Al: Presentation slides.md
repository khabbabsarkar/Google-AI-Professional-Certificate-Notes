## 1. Introduction

Gemini Canvas can turn long documents into clear and professional slide presentations.

You can upload a document, ask Gemini to create slides, edit the design, and customize the presentation for different audiences.

This lab shows how to build, edit, and export a slide deck with Gemini.

## 2. Step 1: Build the Slide Deck

Follow these steps:

1. Open Gemini and click **New chat**.
2. Upload your document using the **Plus (+)** icon.
3. You can upload a file from your device or add one from Google Drive.
4. Open the **Tools** bar.
5. Select **Canvas**.
6. Enter a prompt to create your slide deck.

You can use your own document or a sample document about an IT company's services.

### Example Prompt

```text
Act as an expert presentation designer.

Create a text-only slide deck based on
the document I have attached.

Requirements:
- Identify the most important information.
- Create a clear slide structure.
- Use short and simple sentences.
- Keep the information accurate.
- Do not add information that is not in the document.
- Make the slides easy to understand.
```

### Check AI Accuracy

Always compare the slides with the original document.

AI may:
- Miss important details.
- Change the meaning of information.
- Add incorrect information (hallucination).
- Leave out important facts.

If Gemini misses something, use this prompt:

```text
You missed [specific detail] from the attached
source document.

Please add this information to Slide [number].
Keep the information accurate and clear.
```

## 3. Step 2: Edit Your Slides

After creating the slide deck, you can ask Gemini to make changes.

You can update slide content, colors, tone, tables, and other details.

### Example Editing Prompts

**Update a slide:**

```text
Update the last slide to say:
[Write the new content here].
```

**Change the tone:**

```text
Make the tone of the entire slide deck
more enthusiastic and sales-focused.
Keep the information accurate.
```

**Add information to a table:**

```text
Add a new column to the table on Slide 5.

The new column should include:
[specific data].
```

You can continue editing until the presentation meets your needs.

## 4. Step 3: Customize for Different Audiences

One presentation may not work for everyone.

Gemini can change the same slide deck for different audiences.

For example, a presentation about an IT company can become:

- A client presentation.
- An internal team presentation.
- A new employee onboarding presentation.
- A management presentation.
- A marketing presentation.

### Example Prompt

```text
Change the current slide deck into
an internal onboarding presentation
for new employees.

Requirements:
- Use only the information already provided
  in the slides.
- Use simple and clear language.
- Focus on information that new employees need.
- Keep the slide structure logical.
- Do not add unsupported information.
```

**Important:** Always check the updated slides against the original source.

## 5. Step 4: Export Your Presentation

When you are happy with the final slides, you can export them.

Available options in this lab include:

- **Google Slides:** Continue editing and share the presentation with your team.
- **PDF:** Download the presentation as a PDF file.

Review the exported file to make sure everything looks correct.

## 6. Real-Life Cybersecurity Example

### Create a Cybersecurity Awareness Presentation

Imagine you have a long document about an organization's cybersecurity policy.

You want to explain the policy to employees.

You can use Gemini Canvas to create a slide deck covering:

1. Introduction to cybersecurity.
2. Password security.
3. Phishing awareness.
4. Safe internet browsing.
5. Data protection.
6. Incident reporting.
7. Security best practices.

You can then customize the presentation:

| Audience | Presentation Focus |
|---|---|
| Employees | Basic security rules and safe practices |
| SOC Analysts | Security monitoring and incident reporting |
| IT Team | Technical security requirements |
| Management | Business risks and security priorities |

This makes it easier to communicate cybersecurity information to different groups.

## 8. Key Notes

- Gemini Canvas can create slide decks from uploaded documents.
- Clear prompts help Gemini create better presentations.
- You can edit slide content, tone, colors, and tables.
- One slide deck can be customized for different audiences.
- Always check AI-generated content against the original document.
- You can export the final presentation to Google Slides or PDF.

AI can save time when creating presentations, but accuracy still depends on careful human review.
