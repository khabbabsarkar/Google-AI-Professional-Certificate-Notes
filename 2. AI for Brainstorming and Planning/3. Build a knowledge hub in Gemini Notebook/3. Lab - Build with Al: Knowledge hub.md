## 1. What Is This Lab About?

In this lab, you will learn how to use Gemini Notebook to create a knowledge hub from your project documents.

A knowledge hub is a central place where people can find important project information.

Instead of searching through many files, you can ask Gemini questions in normal language and get answers based on your uploaded sources.

You can also share your notebook with team members so everyone can access the same project information.

## 2. What Will You Learn?

In this lab, you will learn two main things:

1. Ask questions about project documents.
2. Share your notebook with your team.

## 3. Lab Practice: Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Ask Questions About Your Project Documents

First, sign in to Gemini Notebook using your Google Account.

Follow these steps:

1. Open Gemini Notebook.
2. Select **Create a new notebook**.
3. Add your project documents.
4. You can upload files, search the web, or find files from Google Drive.
5. Wait for the documents to appear in your notebook.
6. Open the notebook and ask questions in the text box.

If you do not have your own documents, the lab provides sample materials:

- **Memo:** Explains the project and its main information.
- **Work-back plan:** Shows tasks and deadlines.
- **Packaging plan:** Explains packaging requirements.
- **Creative brief:** Explains the creative direction and goals.

You can add more documents later from the left side of the notebook.

**Example questions:**

```text
When are we starting full-scale packaging production?
```

```text
What are the key milestones of this project?
```

```text
What are the main project goals?
```

```text
Which tasks must be completed before the project launch?
```

**Important: Check the citations**

Gemini Notebook can provide citations with its answers.

These citations link to the original documents used to create the answer.

Why are they useful?

- You can check where the information came from.
- You can verify important details.
- You can find the original information quickly.
- You can reduce the risk of using incorrect information.

### Step 2: Share Your Notebook with Your Team

After creating your knowledge hub, you can share it with other team members.

Follow these steps:

1. Open your notebook.
2. Click the **Share** button at the top of the page.
3. Enter the email addresses of your team members.
4. Add a Welcome Note if needed.
5. Choose the correct access level.
6. Keep the **Notify people** option checked if you want them to receive an email.
7. Click **Save**.

Your teammates will receive an email with a link to the notebook.

**Example Welcome Note:**

```text
Welcome to our project knowledge hub!

You can use this notebook to find important
project information, review documents, and
ask questions about the project.

Please use the available sources to stay
updated and follow the latest project information.
```

## 4. Cybersecurity Example

Imagine you are creating a knowledge hub for a Security Operations Center (SOC) team.

You collect these documents:

- Incident response policy.
- Phishing investigation guide.
- SIEM user guide.
- Vulnerability management policy.
- Security incident reports.

You add these documents to Gemini Notebook.

Now, team members can ask questions such as:

**Question 1:**

```text
What steps should an analyst follow after
detecting a phishing email?
```

**Question 2:**

```text
What is the process for escalating a
critical security incident?
```

**Question 3:**

```text
What are the main steps for investigating
a suspicious login?
```

Gemini Notebook can find relevant information from the uploaded documents and provide answers with citations.

You can then share the notebook with authorized team members.

*This is a hypothetical learning example. Real SOC teams must use approved tools and protect sensitive security information.*

## 5. Complete Workflow

```text
Sign in to Gemini Notebook
          ↓
Create a New Notebook
          ↓
Upload Project Documents
          ↓
Ask Questions About the Sources
          ↓
Review Answers and Citations
          ↓
Share the Notebook with Your Team
          ↓
Team Members Access Information
```

## 6. Important Things to Remember

- Add relevant project documents.
- Use updated and reliable sources.
- Ask clear questions.
- Check citations before trusting important answers.
- Give team members the correct access level.
- Share notebooks only with authorized people.
- Review access when team members change.
- Do not upload confidential documents to unapproved AI tools.


Gemini Notebook helps you turn project documents into a shared knowledge hub.
You can ask questions, find answers with source citations, and share information with your team.

**The main idea: Upload documents → Ask questions → Check citations → Share the notebook → Work with the same information.**
