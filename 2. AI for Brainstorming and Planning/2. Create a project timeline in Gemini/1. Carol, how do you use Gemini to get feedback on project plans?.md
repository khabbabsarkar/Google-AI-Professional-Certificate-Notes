## 1. What Is This About?

Gemini can help you review a project plan and find problems before you start working on it.

Think of Gemini as a smart friend who checks your ideas and asks difficult questions.

You may have a good idea, but you might miss some important details. Gemini can help you find those gaps and improve your plan.

**Example:**

Imagine you want to build a new feature for an app. You write down:
- What the feature will do.
- Why you want to build it.
- Who will use it.
- What resources you need.
- How long it may take.

Then, you share your plan with Gemini and ask for feedback.

Gemini can point out possible problems, risks, and missing details.

## 2. How Can Gemini Help?

You can use Gemini to:

1. **Find weaknesses:** Identify problems in your plan.
2. **Find risks:** Point out things that may go wrong.
3. **Ask difficult questions:** Help you think about things you may have missed.
4. **Suggest improvements:** Give ideas to make your plan better.
5. **Review requirements:** Check whether your plan meets its main goals.

## 3. What Is a Product Requirements Document (PRD)?

A Product Requirements Document (PRD) is a document that explains what a product or feature should do.

It usually includes:
- **Purpose:** Why are you building it?
- **Features:** What will it do?
- **Users:** Who will use it?
- **Requirements:** What does it need to work?
- **Timeline:** How long will it take?
- **Risks:** What problems may happen?

You can upload a PRD to Gemini and ask it to review the plan.

## 4. Useful Prompts for Gemini

**Prompt 1: Review my project plan**

```text
Act as an experienced project manager.

Review my project plan and explain:
1. What is good about this plan?
2. What are its weaknesses?
3. What important details are missing?
4. What risks should I consider?
5. How can I improve it?

Give your feedback in simple English.
```

**Prompt 2: Find problems in my idea**

```text
Act as a critical reviewer.

Look at my project idea and tell me why it might fail.

Find possible problems, risks, missing requirements, and challenges.

Do not only agree with my idea. Give honest and useful feedback.
```

**Prompt 3: Improve my project plan**

```text
Review my project plan and suggest practical improvements.

Focus on the timeline, resources, risks, requirements, and expected results.

Present your suggestions in a clear table.
```

## 5. Cybersecurity Example

Imagine you are planning a new feature for a Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) system.

The feature will automatically check security alerts and help analysts find important threats faster.

Before development, you prepare a PRD and upload it to Gemini.

You ask:

```text
Act as a cybersecurity project reviewer.

Review this plan for an AI-powered SIEM alert triage feature.

Find:
1. Possible security risks.
2. False positive and false negative problems.
3. Data privacy concerns.
4. Integration challenges.
5. Testing requirements.
6. Ways to improve the feature.

Explain your feedback in simple English.
```

Gemini may identify problems such as:
- Too many false alerts may waste analysts' time.
- Some real threats may be missed.
- The feature may not work well with existing security tools.
- Sensitive security data may need extra protection.
- More testing may be needed before deployment.

You can use this feedback to improve the plan before development.

*This is a hypothetical cybersecurity example.*

## 6. A Simple Workflow

```text
Create a Project Idea
        ↓
Write a Project Plan or PRD
        ↓
Upload It to Gemini
        ↓
Ask for Feedback
        ↓
Find Weaknesses and Risks
        ↓
Improve the Plan
        ↓
Review and Finalize
```

## 7. Important Things to Remember

- Do not ask Gemini only to praise your idea.
- Ask it to find problems and challenge your assumptions.
- Give it enough information about your project.
- Check its suggestions before making changes.
- Do not blindly trust its answers.
- Avoid uploading confidential company documents or sensitive information to unapproved AI tools.

Gemini is not just a tool for creating plans. It can also help you test and improve them.

The goal is simple: **Find problems early, improve your ideas, and make better decisions before taking action.**

AI can support your planning, but the final decision should always be yours.
