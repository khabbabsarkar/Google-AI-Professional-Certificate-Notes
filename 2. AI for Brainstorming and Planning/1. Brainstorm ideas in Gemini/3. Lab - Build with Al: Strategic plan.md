## 1. Introduction

Gemini can help turn a business idea into a strategic plan.

A strategic plan helps you decide:
- What you want to achieve.
- Which ideas are worth developing.
- What resources you need.
- How to compare different options.
- What actions you should take next.

In this lab, you will use Gemini to brainstorm product ideas, compare them, research industry trends, and automate regular research.

**Before starting:** Sign in to Gemini using your Google Account.

## 2. Step 1: Brainstorm New Product Ideas

First, ask Gemini to generate ideas for your product or project.

You can give Gemini a role, explain your industry, describe your product, and tell it what kind of ideas you need.

### Prompt Template

```text
You are a [persona] in the [industry].

My company is developing a [new product].

Help me brainstorm [ideas or items]
that I could [purpose].

Generate 10 creative and practical ideas.

For each idea, explain:
1. Description
2. Main benefits
3. Target audience
4. Possible challenges
```

### Cybersecurity Example

```text
You are a cybersecurity education expert.

I want to develop a cybersecurity learning
program for university students.

Help me brainstorm 10 practical learning
activities that can improve their security skills.

Focus on phishing awareness, networking,
ethical hacking, and digital forensics.
```

Review the ideas and select the most interesting options for further evaluation.

## 3. Step 2: Create a Cost-Benefit Analysis Table

After generating ideas, compare them using a cost-benefit analysis.

Cost-benefit analysis means comparing the resources needed for an idea with the benefits it may provide.

### Prompt Template

```text
Perform a cost-benefit analysis for each
of the ideas generated above.

Create a table with these columns:

1. Idea
2. Estimated Cost (Low/Medium/High)
3. Expected Benefit (Low/Medium/High)
4. Difficulty
5. Main Challenges
6. Recommendation

Identify the ideas that offer high benefits
at a low cost.

Explain your reasoning.
```

### Example Evaluation

| Idea | Cost | Benefit | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phishing awareness workshop | Low | High | High |
| Paid security platform | High | High | Medium |
| Free online security guides | Low | Medium | High |
| Large cybersecurity conference | High | High | Medium |

<text color="secondary" size="xs">Illustrative example only. Actual costs and benefits depend on the project and available resources.</text>

**Key Point:** Look for ideas that provide strong benefits without requiring too many resources.

## 4. Step 3: Research Industry Trends

Before making a final decision, understand what is happening in your industry.

Gemini can help you explore:
- New technologies.
- Market changes.
- Customer needs.
- Competitor activities.
- Industry opportunities.
- Emerging risks.

### Prompt Template

```text
What are the latest trends in [industry or topic]?

Focus on:
1. New developments
2. Emerging technologies
3. Important industry challenges
4. Future opportunities
5. Possible risks

Use recent and reliable sources.
Include publication dates and source links.

Explain how these trends may affect
my project or business.
```

### Cybersecurity Example

```text
Research current cybersecurity industry trends.

Focus on:
1. AI-powered cyberattacks
2. AI security
3. Cloud security
4. Threat detection and SOC operations
5. Cybersecurity skills in demand

Use recent and reliable sources.
Include publication dates and links.

Explain which trends may create
learning and career opportunities.
```

**Important:** Verify current trends using reliable sources. AI-generated research may contain outdated or incorrect information.

## 5. Step 4: Schedule a Recurring Analysis

Researching industry trends once is not always enough.

You can use Gemini's Scheduled Actions feature, when available, to repeat a research task automatically.

For example, you can schedule weekly research about cybersecurity developments.

### Prompt Template

```text
Run this industry trend analysis once a week.

Research the latest developments in cybersecurity.

Include:
1. Important new trends
2. Recent security developments
3. Emerging technologies
4. Important industry reports
5. Useful learning opportunities

Provide source links and publication dates.

Summarize the findings in a clear report.
```

### Managing Scheduled Actions

After Gemini confirms the scheduled action:

1. Select **Edit** to change its name or schedule.
2. Review the scheduled action.
3. Save the changes when ready.
4. Find future reports in your conversation list.

To manage existing actions:

1. Open **Settings & help**.
2. Select **Scheduled actions**.
3. Review or edit your scheduled tasks.

<text color="secondary" size="xs">Availability and scheduling options may depend on your Gemini account, plan, and region.</text>

## 7. Important Lessons

| Step | Main Purpose |
|---|---|
| Brainstorm | Generate different ideas. |
| Evaluate | Compare ideas using clear criteria. |
| Cost-Benefit Analysis | Understand costs and expected benefits. |
| Trend Research | Understand industry developments. |
| Scheduled Analysis | Automate regular research. |
| Final Review | Make informed human decisions. |

## Key Notes

- Gemini can help turn ideas into practical plans.
- Use clear prompts to generate useful ideas.
- Compare options using cost-benefit analysis.
- Research industry trends before making decisions.
- Scheduled Actions can automate recurring research.
- Always verify AI-generated information.
- Human judgment is essential when selecting a strategy.
