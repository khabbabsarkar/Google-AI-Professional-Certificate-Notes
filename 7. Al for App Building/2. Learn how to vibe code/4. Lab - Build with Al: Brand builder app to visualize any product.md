In this lab, you will learn how to build a **Brand Builder App** using Google AI Studio.

The app helps you visualize one product across different marketing platforms, such as:

- Billboards
- Newspapers
- Social media posts
- Product advertisements

The main goal is to keep the product looking consistent across all images.

## Before You Start

1. Sign in to Google AI Studio using your Google Account.
2. Open the **Build AI Apps** section.
3. Make sure you understand the free-tier limits.

### Free-Tier Tips

If you see a usage error or payment request:

- Check that you selected a free-tier model.
- Check if you are using a paid feature, such as image or video generation.
- Check whether you have reached your daily usage limit.

Remember, not every model or feature is available for free.

## Step 1: Define Your App Prototype

First, describe the app you want to build.

Open the Build page and enter this prompt:

### Brand Builder App Prompt

```text
I want an app called "Brand Builder App".

The app should let users describe a product
and visualize it across different marketing
platforms, such as:

1. Billboards
2. Newspaper advertisements
3. Social media posts
4. Product advertisements

Maintain the same product design, shape,
colors, and important details across all images.

Do not include any people in the images.

Use the Nano Banana model for image generation.

Create a simple and user-friendly interface
with a product description field and a Generate button.
```

Click the **Build** button.

AI Studio will generate the first version of your app.

## Step 2: Run an Initial Product Test

After the app interface loads, test it with a product idea.

Enter this example into the product description field:

```text
Augmented Reality AI Glasses
```

Then click the **Generate** button.

The app should create different marketing images featuring the same product.

Check the results:

- Does the product look the same in every image?
- Are the colors and design consistent?
- Are there any unwanted people?
- Does each image match its marketing platform?

If image generation fails, move to the next step.

## Step 3: Troubleshoot Errors with AI

Sometimes, an app may try to use different AI models for the same task. This can cause errors or unexpected results.

You can ask AI Studio to review and fix the problem.

### Troubleshooting Process

1. Find the error button at the bottom-right of the window.
2. Open the error section.
3. Select and copy the error messages.
4. Paste the errors into the prompt box on the left.
5. Add clear instructions about what needs to be fixed.

### Corrective Prompt

```text
Please review the errors above and fix the
problems in the app.

Make sure you do not use any other image models.

Use only Gemini 2.5 Flash or Nano Banana
for the required steps, where supported.

Keep the existing app features working.

After fixing the code, check that image generation
works correctly.
```

Click **Send prompt**.

AI Studio will try to update the app based on your instructions.

**Important:** Follow the model names and availability shown in your AI Studio interface. A prompt cannot make a paid model or feature available on the free tier.

## Step 4: Visualize the Marketing Campaign

After fixing the errors:

1. Enter your product idea again.
2. Click the **Generate** button.
3. Wait for the images to finish generating.
4. Review the image gallery.
5. Check the consistency of the product across all images.

### Final Review Checklist

- [ ] The same product appears in every image.
- [ ] Product colors and design remain consistent.
- [ ] No people appear in the images.
- [ ] Each image matches its marketing platform.
- [ ] The app generates images without errors.
- [ ] All original instructions are followed.

## Practical Example: Cybersecurity Product Branding

Imagine you want to promote a new cybersecurity product called **SecureKey**.

It is a smart security key designed to help users protect their accounts.

You could use the Brand Builder App to create:

| Platform | Visual Idea |
|---|---|
| Billboard | A large advertisement featuring SecureKey |
| Newspaper | A traditional product advertisement |
| Social Media | A promotional post for LinkedIn |
| Product Ad | A clean product-focused advertisement |

### Example Product Description

```text
SecureKey is a modern hardware security key.

It has a compact design with a black and silver
color scheme.

Create different marketing images for billboards,
newspapers, social media, and product advertisements.

Keep the same product design and colors
in every image.

Do not include people.
```

This example shows how one product idea can be turned into several marketing assets.

## Key Notes

- Google AI Studio can help you build custom AI-powered apps.
- A clear prompt helps AI understand your app idea.
- Product consistency is important when creating multiple marketing images.
- You can copy error messages and ask AI to fix the app.
- Always test the app before using or sharing its results.
- Check model availability and usage limits before generating images.
