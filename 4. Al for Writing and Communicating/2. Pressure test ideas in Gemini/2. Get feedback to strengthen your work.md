## Introduction

Gemini can help you improve your ideas by looking at them from different points of view.

In this lab, Clara from Gemini Marketing shows how to use AI to act like different stakeholders.

A stakeholder is a person or group who has an interest in a project or decision.

For example:
- A project manager
- A customer
- A company manager
- A technical expert
- A security analyst

You can give Gemini a specific role and background. Then, ask it to review your proposal and give feedback.

## What Can You Learn?

### 1. Increased Confidence

Gemini can help you prepare for difficult questions before an important meeting.

### 2. Stronger Proposals

It can find missing details, weak points, and possible problems in your ideas.

### 3. Different Viewpoints

You can ask different AI personas to review the same proposal.

This helps you understand how different people may react to your ideas.

## How Does It Work?

You can follow these steps:

1. Prepare a draft proposal.
2. Upload the document to Gemini.
3. Give Gemini a stakeholder persona.
4. Ask it to review your proposal.
5. Read the feedback and identify weak points.
6. Ask another persona to review the same proposal.
7. Improve your proposal using the useful feedback.

## Example

Imagine you are preparing a proposal for a new security monitoring system for an organization.

You can ask Gemini to review the proposal from different perspectives.

| Stakeholder | What They May Focus On |
|---|---|
| Security Manager | Security risks and business needs |
| SOC Analyst | Alert quality and daily operations |
| IT Manager | System integration and technical needs |
| Finance Manager | Budget and cost |
| Compliance Officer | Privacy and regulatory requirements |

Each stakeholder may identify different problems.

For example:
- A SOC analyst may find that the system could create too many false alerts.
- An IT manager may identify integration problems.
- A finance manager may question the cost.
- A compliance officer may raise data privacy concerns.

This helps you improve the proposal before presenting it.

## Practical Gemini Prompts

### Prompt 1: Review a Proposal as a Security Manager

```text
Act as an experienced cybersecurity manager.

Review the attached cybersecurity project proposal.

Evaluate it based on:

1. Security benefits
2. Possible risks
3. Technical requirements
4. Missing information
5. Project weaknesses

Be honest and critical.
Do not simply agree with my ideas.

Give practical suggestions to improve the proposal.
```

### Prompt 2: Review the Proposal from Different Perspectives

```text
Review this cybersecurity proposal from the following perspectives:

1. SOC Analyst
2. IT Manager
3. Finance Manager
4. Compliance Officer

For each role, explain:

- Main concerns
- Possible risks
- Missing information
- Questions they may ask during a meeting
- Suggestions for improvement

Present the results in a table.
```

### Prompt 3: Improve the Proposal

```text
Based on the feedback, help me improve the proposal.

Focus on:

- Fixing weak points
- Addressing stakeholder concerns
- Adding missing details
- Making the proposal clearer
- Improving the action plan

Do not invent facts, costs, or technical details.
Mark information that needs further research.
```

## Important Tips

- Give Gemini a clear persona and enough context.
- Upload the correct and latest version of your document.
- Ask it to challenge your ideas instead of only praising them.
- Compare feedback from different personas.
- Use your own judgment when accepting suggestions.
- Verify important technical and factual information.

**Remember:** AI-generated personas are simulations, not real stakeholders or professional experts. Their feedback can help you prepare, but real people may have different opinions.

## Key Notes

- Gemini can simulate different stakeholder perspectives.
- It can identify weaknesses and missing details.
- Multiple personas can provide different viewpoints.
- Feedback can help you improve proposals before important meetings.
- Human judgment is still necessary.
