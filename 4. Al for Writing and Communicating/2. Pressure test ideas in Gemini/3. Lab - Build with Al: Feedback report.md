## Introduction

Gemini can help you review documents, find missing information, and improve your work.

In this lab, you will learn how to give Gemini a specific persona and ask for feedback from that person's point of view.

You will also learn how to discuss possible changes and create a short summary of your improved plan.

## Lab Requirements

- Sign in to Gemini using your Google Account.
- Prepare a document that needs feedback.
- Start a new chat with Gemini.
- If you do not have your own document, use the sample proposal from the lab.

## Step 1: Give Gemini a Persona to Get Feedback

First, click **New chat** in Gemini.

Attach the document you want to review.

Then, give Gemini a specific role and explain what you want it to check.

### Example Prompt

```text
I am working on a cybersecurity project proposal
for a new security monitoring system.

Act as an experienced cybersecurity manager.
Your main priorities are security, risk management,
technical requirements, and project success.

Review the attached document.

From your perspective:
1. What information is missing?
2. What parts are unclear?
3. What risks have not been discussed?
4. What questions would you ask before approving it?

Give clear and practical feedback.
Do not simply agree with my ideas.
```

### What to Do Next

Read Gemini's feedback carefully.

Identify the missing information and important concerns.

You will use this feedback in the next step.

## Step 2: Work Through Potential Edits

Now, discuss possible changes with Gemini in the same chat.

Explain how you plan to fix the problems and ask whether your solution addresses the concerns.

### Example Prompt

```text
You mentioned that my cybersecurity proposal
does not include a clear implementation timeline.

I plan to add:
- Project phases
- Key milestones
- Estimated completion dates
- Required resources
- Responsible team members

Does this resolve your concern?

Is there any other information you would like
me to include?
```

### Why Is This Useful?

You do not need to accept every suggestion immediately.

You can discuss the feedback, suggest solutions, and ask follow-up questions.

This makes the review process more interactive.

## Step 3: Draft a Summary Based on the New Details

After discussing the changes, ask Gemini to create a short summary of the improved plan.

### Example Prompt

```text
Write a two-sentence summary of my updated
cybersecurity project proposal.

The summary is for a one-page project document.

Include:
- The main project goal
- The key improvements
- The expected benefits

Use simple and professional language.
Do not add unsupported claims.
```

You can use Gemini's response as a starting point and edit your original document.

## Cybersecurity Use Cases

This workflow can help with different cybersecurity documents.

| Document | Possible AI Review |
|---|---|
| Security Assessment Report | Find missing risks and findings |
| Incident Response Plan | Review response steps and responsibilities |
| Vulnerability Report | Check severity, evidence, and recommendations |
| Security Awareness Proposal | Review audience, topics, and activities |
| SIEM Implementation Plan | Review technical needs, cost, and risks |
| Penetration Testing Report | Check findings, evidence, and remediation steps |

## Complete Workflow

1. **Upload:** Attach your document to Gemini.
2. **Assign a Persona:** Give Gemini a specific stakeholder role.
3. **Get Feedback:** Ask it to identify missing information.
4. **Discuss Changes:** Suggest edits and ask follow-up questions.
5. **Create a Summary:** Generate a short summary of the updated plan.
6. **Review and Edit:** Check the suggestions and update your document.

## Important Tips

- Give Gemini a clear role and specific priorities.
- Provide enough project context.
- Ask questions about missing information.
- Discuss possible solutions instead of accepting feedback blindly.
- Do not allow Gemini to invent facts, costs, or deadlines.
- Verify important technical information.
- Protect confidential documents and follow your organization's AI policies.

## Key Notes

- Gemini can review documents from different stakeholder perspectives.
- Personas help you receive more targeted feedback.
- You can discuss potential changes with Gemini.
- AI can help create a clear summary of an improved plan.
- Always review and verify AI-generated suggestions.
