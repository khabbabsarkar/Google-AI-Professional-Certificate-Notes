## Introduction

Gemini can help turn a meeting transcript into a clear and useful meeting report.

It can also review the report and check if it is accurate, clear, and complete.

This helps teams:
- Save time.
- Understand meeting discussions.
- Track action items.
- Make better decisions.
- Improve teamwork and productivity.

## Lab Requirements

- Sign in to Gemini using your Google Account.
- Start a new chat.
- Prepare a meeting transcript.
- Use the prompts from the lab and customize them.

If you do not have a transcript, you can use the example transcript provided in the lab.

## Step 1: Generate an Action Item Report

First, open Gemini and click **New chat**.

Attach your meeting transcript and use a prompt to create a report.

### Example Prompt

```text
Act as a senior project manager.

Create a meeting report based on the attached transcript.

Include these sections:

1. Meeting Summary
2. Main Discussion Points
3. Important Decisions
4. Action Items
5. Responsible People
6. Deadlines
7. Follow-up Tasks

Use simple and clear language.

Do not add information that is not in the transcript.
If an owner or deadline is missing, write "Not specified".
```

### Why Is This Useful?

Gemini can find important information from a long transcript.

It can organize the information into a report that is easier to read and share.

## Step 2: Evaluate the Report

After Gemini creates the report, review its quality.

Use another prompt in the same chat to check the report.

### Example Prompt

```text
Evaluate the meeting report using these criteria:

1. Brevity: Is the report short and focused?
2. Clear Action Items: Are the tasks easy to understand?
3. Clarity: Is the language simple and clear?
4. Accuracy: Does the report match the original transcript?
5. Completeness: Are important decisions and tasks included?

Identify any missing or incorrect information.

Suggest improvements and explain how to make the report better.
Do not invent missing details.
```

### What Should You Check?

- Does the report include the main points?
- Are the action items clear?
- Are the responsible people correctly identified?
- Are the deadlines correct?
- Does the report contain information that was not in the transcript?

Always compare the report with the original transcript.

## Cybersecurity Example

Imagine a cybersecurity team has a meeting about suspicious network activity.

The team discusses:
- Unusual login attempts.
- Possible phishing activity.
- Security alerts from the SIEM.
- Vulnerabilities that need investigation.
- Tasks for the next security review.

You can upload the meeting transcript to Gemini and ask it to create a structured report.

### Example Prompt

```text
Act as a SOC team coordinator.

Create a meeting summary based on the attached cybersecurity meeting transcript.

Include:

1. Main Security Issues
2. Suspicious Activities Discussed
3. Important Decisions
4. Investigation Tasks
5. Responsible Team Members
6. Deadlines
7. Follow-up Actions

Use clear and simple language.

Do not assume that suspicious activity is a confirmed attack.
Do not invent names, deadlines, or technical findings.
Mark missing information as "Not specified".
```

This can help a SOC team organize discussions and follow up on important security tasks.

## Important Safety Tips

- Use only approved AI tools for meeting transcripts.
- Do not upload confidential company information to public AI tools without permission.
- Share reports only with authorized people.
- Check the report before sharing it.
- Keep human review in the process.

## Key Notes

- Gemini can turn meeting transcripts into structured reports.
- It can identify decisions, action items, owners, and deadlines.
- It can evaluate a report using clear criteria.
- Always check the report against the original transcript.
- AI can support teamwork, but humans are responsible for final decisions.
