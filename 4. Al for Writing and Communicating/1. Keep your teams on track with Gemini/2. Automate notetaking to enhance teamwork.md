## 1. Overview

AI tools like Gemini can help us take meeting notes and turn them into clear action plans.

During a meeting, it can be difficult to listen carefully, join the discussion, and write down every important detail at the same time.

Gemini in Google Meet can help capture meeting notes. We can also use Gemini to organize those notes into a structured action plan.

This helps teams understand what was discussed, what decisions were made, and what needs to happen next.

## 2. What Can AI Help Us Do?

### A. Focused Meetings

We can focus more on the discussion instead of writing every detail.

AI can help capture important points from the meeting.

### B. Actionable Next Steps

Gemini can turn meeting notes into an action plan.

The plan can include:
- Tasks to complete.
- Person responsible for each task.
- Deadlines.
- Important decisions.
- Follow-up activities.

### C. Better Team Alignment

Everyone can use the same meeting summary and action plan.

This reduces confusion and helps team members understand their responsibilities.

## 3. How the Process Works

The basic workflow is:

1. **Start the meeting:** Discuss the topic with your team.
2. **Capture notes:** Use Gemini in Google Meet to help take meeting notes, where available.
3. **Review the notes:** Check the captured information.
4. **Generate an action plan:** Ask Gemini to organize decisions and tasks.
5. **Assign responsibilities:** Identify the owner of each task.
6. **Set deadlines:** Add due dates for the tasks.
7. **Share the plan:** Send the final notes to the relevant team members.

## 4. Practical Example: Cybersecurity Team Meeting

Imagine a cybersecurity team holds a meeting about improving network security.

The team discusses:
- Recent security alerts.
- Possible network vulnerabilities.
- Firewall configuration.
- Security monitoring.
- Required security updates.

After the meeting, you can give the transcript to Gemini and ask it to create an action plan.

### Useful Gemini Prompt

> Act as a professional meeting assistant.
>
> Review the meeting transcript and create a structured meeting summary and action plan.
>
> Include:
> 1. Main discussion points.
> 2. Important decisions.
> 3. Tasks that need to be completed.
> 4. The person responsible for each task.
> 5. Deadlines for each task.
> 6. Unresolved questions and follow-up activities.
>
> Present the action plan in a table with these columns:
> - Task
> - Responsible Person
> - Deadline
> - Priority
> - Status
>
> Use only the information from the transcript. Do not invent task owners, deadlines, or decisions. If information is missing, write "Not specified."

## 5. Example Action Plan

Imagine the meeting transcript contains three agreed tasks.

| Task | Responsible Person | Deadline | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Review firewall rules | Security Analyst | 12 November | High |
| Check unresolved security alerts | SOC Analyst | 13 November | High |
| Update security documentation | Team Lead | 15 November | Medium |

<text color="secondary" size="xs">Illustrative example only. Real owners and deadlines should come from the meeting.</text>

## 6. Benefits for Cybersecurity Teams

| Benefit | How It Helps |
|---|---|
| Incident response | Tracks investigation tasks and follow-ups. |
| Vulnerability management | Records who will review and fix vulnerabilities. |
| SOC operations | Organizes alert investigation tasks. |
| Security audits | Keeps records of decisions and required actions. |
| Team collaboration | Helps everyone understand their responsibilities. |

## 7. Important Things to Remember

- Review AI-generated meeting notes for accuracy.
- Make sure important decisions are recorded correctly.
- Never allow AI to invent task owners or deadlines.
- Check that action items match the actual discussion.
- Protect confidential meeting transcripts.
- Use approved tools and follow organizational privacy policies.
- Share meeting notes only with authorized people.

AI-powered note-taking can make meetings more productive.

Instead of spending time writing everything manually, team members can focus on discussions while AI helps organize the information into clear summaries and action plans.
