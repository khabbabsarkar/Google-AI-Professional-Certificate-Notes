## 1. Introduction

AI is changing how we analyze data.

Traditionally, people focus mainly on multiple-choice survey answers. They often read written comments only when the answers are negative or unclear.

But written answers can contain valuable information.

AI helps us study these answers and find useful patterns, ideas, and problems.

## 2. Traditional vs. New Approach

### Traditional Approach

- Focus mainly on multiple-choice answers.
- Calculate average ratings and percentages.
- Read written comments only when something looks wrong.
- May miss useful details from open-text responses.

### New Approach with AI

- Treat all written responses as valuable data.
- Analyze both positive and negative feedback.
- Find common topics and patterns.
- Understand people's opinions in more detail.
- Use these insights to make better decisions.

## 3. What Is Open-Text Data?

Open-text data is information that people write in their own words.

For example, a survey may contain two types of questions:

| Question Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Multiple-choice | How would you rate our training? (1–5) |
| Open-text | What did you like or dislike about the training? |

Multiple-choice answers are easy to count and compare.

Open-text answers provide more detail about people's thoughts, experiences, and problems.

## 4. Why Is Open-Text Data Important?

Written feedback can reveal information that numbers alone cannot show.

For example, imagine 100 employees rate cybersecurity training.

The average rating is 4 out of 5.

This looks like a good result.

But written feedback may reveal:

- Employees want more practical exercises.
- Some people do not understand phishing attacks.
- The training contains too much technical language.
- Employees want real-world security examples.
- Some topics need more explanation.

Without reading these comments, the organization may miss important problems.

## 5. How AI Helps Analyze Survey Data

AI can help us:

1. Read large numbers of written responses.
2. Group similar answers into categories.
3. Identify common problems and suggestions.
4. Find positive and negative opinions.
5. Summarize important feedback.
6. Discover patterns that may need further investigation.

AI does not replace human judgment. People still need to review the results and make decisions.

## 6. Real-Life Cybersecurity Example

### Analyzing Employee Feedback on Cybersecurity Training

Imagine a company provides cybersecurity awareness training to its employees.

After the training, the company collects survey responses.

The survey includes:

- A rating question about training quality.
- A written question asking employees for suggestions.

### Example Survey Responses

| Employee | Rating | Written Feedback |
|---|---:|---|
| Employee 1 | 5 | The phishing examples were very useful. |
| Employee 2 | 4 | I want more hands-on exercises. |
| Employee 3 | 5 | The training was easy to understand. |
| Employee 4 | 3 | I still find suspicious emails difficult to identify. |
| Employee 5 | 4 | Please include more real-world attack examples. |

### What AI Can Discover

After analyzing the written responses, AI may identify these themes:

| Theme | Insight |
|---|---|
| Practical Learning | Employees want more hands-on exercises. |
| Phishing Awareness | Some employees need more help identifying suspicious emails. |
| Real-World Examples | Employees want examples of real cyberattacks. |
| Training Quality | Some employees find the training easy to understand. |

### Possible Actions

Based on these insights, the cybersecurity team could:

- Add practical phishing exercises.
- Show examples of suspicious emails.
- Include real-world attack scenarios.
- Explain difficult security topics in simple language.
- Improve future training sessions.

This approach helps the organization understand employee needs and improve cybersecurity awareness.

## 7. Practical Gemini Prompts

You can use Gemini to analyze open-text survey responses.

### Prompt 1: Find Common Themes

```text
Analyze the following survey responses.

Identify the common themes and topics.

Do not focus only on negative feedback.
Treat every response as valuable information.

Group similar responses together
and explain each theme in simple English.
```

### Prompt 2: Identify Problems and Suggestions

```text
Analyze these employee feedback responses
about cybersecurity awareness training.

Identify:
- Common problems.
- Employee concerns.
- Suggestions for improvement.
- Positive feedback.

Present the results in a table.
Do not invent information.
```

### Prompt 3: Summarize Survey Insights

```text
Review all the survey responses.

Create a short report containing:
1. Main findings.
2. Common themes.
3. Positive feedback.
4. Areas that need improvement.
5. Recommended next steps.

Use clear and simple English.
Support each finding with examples
from the provided responses.
```

## 8. Key Takeaways

- Open-text responses are valuable sources of information.
- Multiple-choice answers do not tell the whole story.
- AI can help analyze large amounts of written feedback.
- Both positive and negative responses deserve attention.
- AI can discover common themes, problems, and suggestions.
- Human review is important before making decisions.

Numbers can show us what people think, but written feedback can help us understand why they think that way.

Do not ignore the details hidden inside open-text responses. They may contain some of the most useful insights in your data.
