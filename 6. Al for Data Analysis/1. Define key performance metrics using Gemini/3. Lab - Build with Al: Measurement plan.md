## 1. Introduction

A measurement plan helps you decide what data to track and how to measure progress toward a goal.

Before preparing a presentation or report, you need to know:

- What is the main goal?
- Which metrics are important?
- What questions might leadership ask?
- Where can you find the required data?

Gemini can help answer these questions and prepare a clear measurement plan.

## 2. Lab Overview

In this lab, you will learn three main steps:

1. Identify key presentation metrics.
2. Anticipate leadership questions.
3. Get guidance on collecting data from a specific source.

Sign in to Gemini with your Google Account to use additional features and save your activity.

## 3. Step 1: Identify Key Presentation Metrics

First, open Gemini and click **New chat**.

Explain your company type, meeting purpose, and main goal.

### Example Prompt

```text
I work at a cybersecurity company.

I am preparing for a monthly
security performance review.

Our goal is to improve threat detection
and incident response.

What are the top 5 metrics I should
consider for my presentation?

For each metric, explain:
- What it measures.
- Why it is important.
- How it supports our goal.

Present the results in a table.
```

Review Gemini's suggestions.

Choose one important metric to explore in the next step.

### Example Metrics for Cybersecurity

| Metric | What It Measures |
|---|---|
| Mean Time to Detect (MTTD) | Average time needed to detect an incident. |
| Mean Time to Respond (MTTR) | Average time needed to respond to an incident. |
| Confirmed Security Incidents | Number of verified security incidents. |
| False Positive Rate | Percentage of alerts that are incorrectly identified as threats. |
| Vulnerability Remediation Rate | Percentage of identified vulnerabilities that have been fixed. |

## 4. Step 2: Anticipate Leadership Questions

Leadership teams may ask questions about the metrics in your presentation.

Gemini can help you prepare for these questions before the meeting.

For example, if you choose **Mean Time to Detect (MTTD)**, leadership may ask:

- Is our detection time improving?
- Why are some incidents taking longer to detect?
- Which types of incidents take the longest to identify?
- How does our detection performance compare with previous months?
- What can we do to improve detection speed?

### Example Prompt

```text
What questions might our leadership team
ask about Mean Time to Detect (MTTD)?

Our goal is to improve threat detection
and incident response.

Suggest 10 possible questions.

For each question, explain:
- Why leadership may ask it.
- What data can help answer it.
- What information I should prepare.

Use simple and clear English.
```

Review the questions and identify the data you need.

## 5. Step 3: Get Data from a Specific Data Source

Sometimes, you know where your data is stored but do not know how to export it.

Gemini can provide step-by-step instructions.

For example, you may want to export website traffic data from Google Analytics into Google Sheets.

### General Prompt

```text
How do I export [type of data]
from [data source] so I can analyze it
in [destination]?

Explain the process step by step.

Use simple English and include
any important settings I should check.
```

### Cybersecurity Example

```text
How do I export security incident data
from a SIEM platform into a CSV file
so I can analyze it in Google Sheets?

Explain the process step by step.

Include:
- How to select a date range.
- How to choose relevant fields.
- How to export the data.
- How to check the exported file.

Do not assume a specific SIEM platform.
Ask me which platform I use if needed.
```

**Note:** Export options depend on the platform, user permissions, and organization policies. Never export confidential security data to an unapproved tool.

## 6. Real-Life Cybersecurity Example

### Prepare a Monthly SOC Measurement Plan

Imagine you need to prepare a monthly SOC performance presentation.

Your main goal is to understand how well the security team detects and responds to threats.

You can use Gemini to prepare the measurement plan.

| Step | Activity | Expected Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify important SOC metrics. | A list of key measurements. |
| 2 | Select MTTD as a key metric. | A clear reporting focus. |
| 3 | Anticipate leadership questions. | A list of possible questions. |
| 4 | Identify the required data. | Relevant SIEM records. |
| 5 | Export and analyze data. | Calculated metrics and trends. |
| 6 | Prepare the presentation. | A clear monthly SOC report. |

## 8. Best Practices

1. Start with a clear business or project goal.
2. Select metrics that support that goal.
3. Choose measurable and relevant metrics.
4. Think about possible leadership questions.
5. Identify the correct data sources.
6. Check data quality and calculations.
7. Protect confidential information.
8. Verify Gemini's suggestions before using them.

## 9. Key Takeaways

- A measurement plan helps organize data reporting.
- Gemini can suggest important metrics for a presentation.
- AI can help predict questions from leadership.
- Gemini can provide guidance on exporting data.
- Data analysis should support clear goals and decisions.
- Human review and data privacy are essential.

A strong presentation starts before you create the slides. First, know what you want to measure, why it matters, and what questions your audience may ask.
