## 1. Overview

Gemini can help us research a topic and create a detailed research report.

We can use it to:
- Find market trends and new ideas.
- Explore a topic in detail.
- Collect information from different sources.
- Focus on specific questions and time periods.
- Create an infographic from the final report.

**Deep Research** is a Gemini feature that helps research a topic in more detail by reviewing information from multiple sources.

## 2. Lab Requirements

- Sign in to Gemini using your Google Account.
- Open a new chat.
- Use the Deep Research feature.
- Review the research plan before starting.
- Check the final report and its sources.

## 3. Lab Steps

### Step 1: Find Initial Ideas

First, open a new chat in Gemini.

Use this prompt and replace the text inside the brackets:

**Prompt:**

> I'm planning a [new product or service] for my company. What [risks or market trends] should I be aware of?

Review Gemini's answer. Choose an interesting topic for deeper research.

### Step 2: Start a Deep Research Project

1. Open a new chat.
2. Select **Deep Research** from the Tools menu.
3. Enter a research prompt.

**Prompt:**

> I'm planning a [new product or service] for my company. Research [a specific market trend from the previous response].

Gemini will create a **Research Plan**. Review it before moving forward.

### Step 3: Improve the Research Plan

Click **Edit Plan** in the same Deep Research chat.

Use this prompt:

> Focus on [a specific part of the topic]. Use only sources from [a time period, such as the last two years]. Include [specific data, such as customer adoption or market growth].

This helps Gemini focus on the information you need.

### Step 4: Start the Research

Click **Start Research**.

Gemini will research the topic and prepare a detailed report.

Wait a few minutes, then review the report and its sources.

### Step 5: Create an Infographic

After the research is complete:

1. Open the research report.
2. Click **Create** in the top-right corner.
3. Select **Infographic**.
4. Review the generated infographic.

An infographic presents important information in a visual and easy-to-understand way.

## 4. Cybersecurity Example

You can use the same workflow to research cybersecurity topics.

For example, imagine you want to study **AI-powered cyberattacks**.

**Step 1 — Find ideas**

> I am studying cybersecurity. What new AI-related cyber threats should I know about?

**Step 2 — Start Deep Research**

> Research the growing use of AI in cyberattacks, including AI-powered phishing, deepfake scams, and automated vulnerability discovery.

**Step 3 — Refine the plan**

> Focus on threats reported during the last 12 months. Include real-world examples, attack methods, affected industries, and possible security solutions. Use reliable sources and include publication dates and links.

**Step 4 — Start Research**

Review the research plan and click **Start Research**.

**Step 5 — Create an infographic**

Turn the main findings into a cybersecurity awareness infographic.

## 5. Why Is This Useful?

| Benefit | Simple Explanation |
|---|---|
| Saves time | Researches many sources for you. |
| Better insights | Finds useful facts and trends. |
| Focused research | Lets you choose the topic and time period. |
| Detailed reports | Organizes findings into a report. |
| Visual learning | Turns research into an infographic. |

## 6. Important Things to Remember

- Start with a clear research question.
- Review the research plan before starting.
- Give Gemini a specific topic and time period.
- Use reliable sources.
- Check facts, dates, statistics, and source links.
- Do not trust every AI-generated result without checking it.
- Use the report as a starting point for your own analysis.

Gemini Deep Research can help us move from a simple question to a detailed research report and a visual infographic.

In cybersecurity, this can help us study new threats, understand attack methods, explore security trends, and learn about possible defenses.

**Remember:** AI can make research faster, but human thinking and fact-checking are still important.
