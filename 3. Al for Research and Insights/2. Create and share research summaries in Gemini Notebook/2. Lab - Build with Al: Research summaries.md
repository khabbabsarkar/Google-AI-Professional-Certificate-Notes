## 1. Overview

Gemini Notebook helps us turn information from different sources into useful summaries and learning materials.

We can upload documents, PDFs, websites, podcasts, and YouTube videos. Then, we can ask questions and create different types of resources from those sources.

This makes research and learning faster.

## 2. Lab Requirements

- Sign in to Gemini Notebook using your Google Account.
- Create a new notebook.
- Add your research sources.
- Use prompts to summarize and understand the information.

## 3. Lab Steps

### Step 1: Generate a Summary of Your Sources

1. Open Gemini Notebook.
2. Select **Create a new notebook**.
3. Upload your research sources.

You can use different types of sources:
- Podcast
- PDF document
- Website
- YouTube video

You can also use your own documents and learning materials.

After uploading the sources, enter this prompt:

**Prompt:**

> Summarize the key learnings from these sources in [desired length, such as one page].

Gemini Notebook will create a summary based on your uploaded sources.

**Important:** The summary includes citations that link to the original sources. You can use them to check the information and verify the AI-generated content.

### Step 2: Ask Questions About Your Sources

You can ask questions in the text box at the bottom of your notebook.

This helps you:
- Find common ideas across different sources.
- Understand a specific document.
- Explore a topic in more detail.
- Identify which sources discuss a particular subject.

**Example question from the lab:**

> Do any of these sources talk about how to test different color choices in design?

Gemini Notebook will look through your sources and provide an answer.

### Step 3: Generate Additional Learning Resources

Open the **Studio** panel on the right side of your notebook.

You can create different resources by selecting the available options.

Examples include:

| Resource | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Audio Overview | Listen to an audio summary of your sources. |
| Video Overview | Understand information through a video format. |
| Mind Map | See connections between different ideas. |
| Other resources | Create additional learning materials from your sources. |

These formats can help you understand and remember information in different ways.

## 4. Cybersecurity Example

Imagine you want to learn about **AI Security and Prompt Injection**.

You have collected:
- A PDF about the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications.
- A YouTube video explaining prompt injection.
- A research article about LLM security.
- A podcast about AI-related cyber threats.

You can upload all these sources into one Gemini Notebook.

### Prompt 1: Create Research Notes

> Summarize all my uploaded sources about AI security and prompt injection. Use simple English and organize the key ideas with headings and bullet points. Include important attack methods, risks, and defense strategies.

### Prompt 2: Ask Questions

> Based only on my uploaded sources, explain how prompt injection works. What are the common risks, and what security measures can help reduce them? Include citations.

### Prompt 3: Find Common Themes

> Compare all my uploaded sources. Identify the common security risks discussed in them. Highlight any differences in their explanations or recommendations.

### Prompt 4: Create a Mind Map

Use the Studio panel to create a mind map that connects:
- AI Security
- Prompt Injection
- Sensitive Information Disclosure
- Insecure Tool Access
- Excessive Agency
- Security Defenses

## 5. Benefits for Cybersecurity Learners

| Benefit | How It Helps |
|---|---|
| Faster learning | Understand many sources in less time. |
| Better organization | Keep research materials in one notebook. |
| Source-based answers | Find answers using uploaded materials. |
| Easy revision | Create summaries and study notes. |
| Visual learning | Use mind maps and video overviews. |
| Flexible learning | Listen to audio summaries when reading is not convenient. |

## 6. Important Things to Remember

- Upload relevant and trustworthy sources.
- Give clear instructions when asking questions.
- Use citations to check the answers.
- Make sure the summary matches the original sources.
- Do not assume that source-based answers are always correct.
- Protect confidential information when uploading files.

Gemini Notebook can turn multiple sources into summaries, answers, and different learning resources.

It helps us spend less time collecting information and more time understanding it.
