## 1. What Is This About?

Gemini Deep Research helps you explore complex topics, collect information from many sources, and create detailed research reports.

Sometimes, a simple search is not enough to understand a topic.

For example, if you want to understand the current cybersecurity threat landscape, you may need to read many articles, reports, and security advisories.

Gemini Deep Research can help you collect and organize this information into one report.

It also allows you to plan your research before it starts. You can choose what information you need, which sources to use, and what period to focus on.

## 2. What Will You Learn?

This lab focuses on three main benefits.

### A. Comprehensive Answers

Gemini Deep Research can help answer complex questions by studying information from multiple sources.

For example:
- What are the latest cybersecurity threats?
- How is AI changing cybersecurity?
- What are the common risks of cloud computing?
- How are attackers using social engineering?

It can combine useful findings into a detailed report.

### B. Targeted and Relevant Results

You can customize your research plan before generating the report.

You can tell Gemini:
- What topic to research.
- What questions to answer.
- Which sources to focus on.
- What time period to cover.
- What information to include or exclude.

This helps make the research more useful and relevant.

### C. Shareable Insights

After generating a research report, you can turn important findings into an infographic.

An infographic uses images, icons, charts, and short text to explain information.

It helps people understand complex research more easily.

For example, you can create a cybersecurity infographic showing:
- Common cyber threats.
- Important statistics.
- Attack methods.
- Security recommendations.

## 3. How Does Gemini Deep Research Work?

The process has four main stages:

1. **Ask a broad question:** Start with a research topic.
2. **Create a research plan:** Gemini prepares a plan based on your question.
3. **Refine the plan:** Review and change the research focus, sources, and time period.
4. **Generate the report:** Gemini collects and organizes information into a detailed report.

You can then review the findings and create an infographic to share the main ideas.

## 4. Cybersecurity Example: Research on AI-Powered Cyberattacks

Imagine you want to understand how attackers are using AI to improve cyberattacks.

You can start with a broad question.

**Initial research question:**

```text
How is artificial intelligence changing
the cybersecurity threat landscape?
```

Next, give Gemini more specific instructions.

**Refined research prompt:**

```text
Research how artificial intelligence is changing
the cybersecurity threat landscape.

Focus on:
1. AI-powered phishing attacks.
2. Deepfake-based social engineering.
3. Automated vulnerability discovery.
4. AI-assisted malware development.
5. Risks to organizations.
6. Security methods to reduce these risks.

Focus on information published during the
last 12 months.

Prioritize trusted cybersecurity organizations,
security research papers, and official reports.

Include source links, publication dates,
important findings, and practical recommendations.

Organize the results into a detailed report.
```

Gemini can use this research plan to produce a more focused report.

**After generating the report:**

You can ask Gemini to create an infographic.

```text
Create a clear cybersecurity infographic
based on the research report.

Include:
- Five important findings.
- Key statistics with their sources.
- Major AI-powered cyber threats.
- Recommended security practices.

Use simple English and a clean,
professional design.
```

*This is a hypothetical cybersecurity research example. Check current sources and verify statistics before sharing them.*

## 5. Useful Research Planning Tips

Before starting Deep Research, think about these points:

| Research element | What to decide |
|---|---|
| Topic | What do you want to research? |
| Scope | Which areas should be covered? |
| Timeframe | How recent should the information be? |
| Sources | Which websites or reports are reliable? |
| Focus | What questions should the report answer? |
| Output | Do you need a detailed report, summary, or infographic? |

A clear research plan helps AI focus on the information you actually need.

## 6. Complete Workflow

```text
Ask a Broad Research Question
          ↓
Create a Research Plan
          ↓
Review and Refine the Plan
          ↓
Select Sources and Timeframe
          ↓
Generate a Research Report
          ↓
Check Facts and Sources
          ↓
Create an Infographic
          ↓
Share Important Insights
```

## 7. Important Things to Remember

- Start with a clear research question.
- Give Gemini enough context.
- Define the research scope.
- Choose reliable sources.
- Set a suitable timeframe.
- Review the research plan before starting.
- Check the report's facts and citations.
- Verify statistics using original sources.
- Make sure the infographic represents the report correctly.

Gemini Deep Research helps turn complex research questions into detailed and easy-to-understand reports.

You can control the research scope, select useful sources, and turn important findings into shareable infographics.

**The main idea: Ask → Plan → Refine → Research → Verify → Visualize → Share.**
