## 1. Overview

Gemini allows us to create a custom AI expert by giving it a specific role, characteristics, and instructions.

This custom expert can help us:
- Get advice about a specific topic.
- Review and improve our ideas.
- Find weaknesses and possible risks.
- Challenge our thinking.
- Make better decisions.

In this lab, we will explore an expert persona, create a custom AI expert, and use it to get advice.

## 2. Lab Steps

### Step 1: Explore an Expert Persona

First, open a new chat in Gemini.

Use this prompt and replace the text inside the brackets:

**Prompt:**

> What are the key characteristics of [an expert role relevant to your work, such as a cybersecurity analyst]?

Gemini will explain the important skills, knowledge, and qualities of that expert.

Read the response and copy the useful information. You will use it to create your custom AI expert.

### Step 2: Create a Custom Expert in Gemini

Start a new chat and enter the following instructions in the instructions box.

Replace the bracketed text with your own details and the characteristics from Step 1.

**Prompt:**

> You are [expert role]. Your key characteristics include: [characteristics from Gemini's previous response].
>
> I am a [your role].
>
> Use your expertise to challenge my thinking and ensure my ideas are sound.
>
> Your advice should be clear, honest, and informative. Stay in character throughout the conversation.

Gemini will now act according to the expert role and instructions you provided.

### Step 3: Ask Your Custom AI Expert Questions

Start a new chat with your custom AI expert.

You can ask questions, request advice, or get feedback on your ideas.

**Example prompt:**

> What are the top factors to consider when selecting [a product or service related to your work]?

You can also ask the expert to review a plan, identify risks, or suggest improvements.

### Step 4: Pin Your Expert Chat

You can pin the expert chat thread to find and reuse it easily.

Follow these steps:

1. Open the left-side panel in Gemini.
2. Find your expert chat thread.
3. Click the three dots next to the chat.
4. Select the pin icon.

Your expert chat will be easier to access later.

## 3. Cybersecurity Example: Create a Custom AI Security Expert

Let's create an AI expert that can help a cybersecurity learner.

### Step 1: Explore the Persona

**Prompt:**

> What are the key characteristics of a highly skilled cybersecurity analyst who specializes in threat detection, security monitoring, incident response, and risk management?

Gemini may suggest characteristics such as:
- Strong networking and security knowledge.
- Knowledge of SIEM tools.
- Understanding of common cyber threats.
- Analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Knowledge of incident response.
- Attention to detail.
- Critical thinking.

### Step 2: Create the Custom Expert

**Prompt:**

> You are an experienced cybersecurity analyst specializing in threat detection, security monitoring, incident response, and risk management.
>
> Your key characteristics include strong technical knowledge, analytical thinking, problem-solving skills, attention to detail, and a security-focused mindset.
>
> I am a cybersecurity learner interested in penetration testing, digital forensics, SOC operations, and AI security.
>
> Help me understand cybersecurity concepts, review my learning plans, and improve my technical knowledge.
>
> Challenge my assumptions, identify mistakes, and explain your advice using simple English. Use practical examples where helpful. Do not simply agree with everything I say.

### Step 3: Test Your AI Expert

Try asking these questions:

**Question 1: Learning Advice**

> What skills should I focus on to prepare for an entry-level SOC analyst position?

**Question 2: Technical Knowledge**

> Explain how a SOC analyst investigates a suspicious login attempt. Show the process step by step.

**Question 3: Critical Feedback**

> Review my cybersecurity learning plan. Identify missing skills, weaknesses, and areas I should improve.

**Question 4: Career Preparation**

> Ask me five technical interview questions for an entry-level cybersecurity analyst role. Ask one question at a time and evaluate my answers.

## 4. Benefits of Custom AI Experts

| Benefit | Explanation |
|---|---|
| Specialized advice | Get answers based on a specific expert role. |
| Faster feedback | Review ideas in less time. |
| New perspectives | Discover weaknesses and missed opportunities. |
| Better decisions | Explore risks before taking action. |
| Reusable knowledge | Return to a saved expert chat when needed. |

## 5. Important Things to Remember

- Choose an expert role that matches your goal.
- Give clear instructions and enough context.
- Ask the expert to challenge your ideas.
- Test its advice with practical questions.
- Verify important technical information.
- Do not treat a simulated AI persona as a real professional.
- Protect sensitive and confidential information.

Gemini allows us to create custom AI experts for different tasks.

By defining an expert role and giving clear instructions, we can get useful feedback, explore new perspectives, and improve our ideas.
