## 1. Overview

Gemini can act like a custom AI expert when we give it clear instructions and an expert role.

We can create an AI expert for different topics, such as cybersecurity, business, marketing, education, and technology.

This expert can give advice, review ideas, find weaknesses, and help us look at problems from a different point of view.

**Example:** We can ask Gemini to act as a cybersecurity expert and review a security plan.

## 2. What Can a Custom AI Expert Do?

### Better Ideas, Faster

A custom AI expert can review our ideas and give useful feedback in a short time.

### New Perspectives

It can look at a problem from an expert's point of view.

It can also:
- Find weaknesses in our ideas.
- Identify possible risks.
- Challenge our assumptions.
- Suggest better solutions.
- Show problems we may have missed.

### Shared Knowledge

We can create a collection of AI experts for different tasks.

A team can use these experts to get advice and feedback whenever needed, depending on the sharing features available.

## 3. How to Create a Custom AI Expert

To create a useful AI expert, we should provide:

1. **Persona:** Tell Gemini which expert role to take.
2. **Goal:** Explain what we want the expert to do.
3. **Context:** Give background information about the problem.
4. **Instructions:** Explain how the expert should review the topic.
5. **Output Format:** Tell Gemini how to present the results.

A clear role and detailed instructions can help Gemini give more useful answers.

## 4. Cybersecurity Example: Create an AI Security Expert

Imagine you are planning a cybersecurity awareness program for university students.

You want feedback from someone with cybersecurity knowledge.

You can ask Gemini to act as a cybersecurity expert.

### Custom Expert Prompt

> Act as an experienced cybersecurity awareness and security education expert.
>
> Your task is to review my ideas for a cybersecurity awareness program for university students.
>
> Focus on phishing attacks, password security, social engineering, data privacy, and safe internet use.
>
> Review my ideas and identify:
> - Weak points.
> - Possible risks.
> - Missing topics.
> - Problems with the target audience.
> - Ways to improve the program.
>
> Be honest and critical. Do not agree with every idea. Explain your suggestions in simple English and organize your feedback using headings and bullet points.

This custom expert can help us improve the awareness program before using it.

## 5. Other Cybersecurity Use Cases

| Expert Persona | What It Can Help With |
|---|---|
| Penetration Testing Expert | Review authorized lab testing plans and identify missing steps. |
| SOC Analyst | Review hypothetical security alerts and suggest investigation steps. |
| Digital Forensics Expert | Review forensic investigation plans and evidence-handling procedures. |
| Incident Response Expert | Review incident response plans and identify possible gaps. |
| AI Security Expert | Review AI application risks, such as prompt injection and data exposure. |
| Cybersecurity Career Coach | Review learning plans, CVs, and interview preparation. |

## 6. Useful Prompt: Challenge My Ideas

You can use this prompt with almost any custom AI expert:

> Act as a critical expert in [your chosen field].
>
> Review my idea: [describe your idea].
>
> Do not simply agree with me. Challenge my assumptions and look for weaknesses.
>
> Identify possible risks, missing information, and areas for improvement. Suggest practical solutions.
>
> If you need more information, ask me questions before giving your final feedback.
>
> Present your response in simple English with clear headings and bullet points.

## 7. Responsible Use of AI Experts

AI experts are simulated personas. They are not real professionals and do not have guaranteed expert knowledge.

Keep these points in mind:

- Check important facts and recommendations.
- Do not blindly trust AI-generated advice.
- Protect private and confidential information.
- Use approved sources and tools for professional work.
- Ask a real professional when expert judgment is required.
- Take responsibility for your final decisions.

In cybersecurity, always verify technical advice before applying it to real systems.

A custom AI expert can help us think differently, find weaknesses, and improve our decisions.

Instead of asking AI only to agree with our ideas, we can ask it to challenge them.
