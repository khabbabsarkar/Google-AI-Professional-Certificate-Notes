## 1. Introduction

AI can help us complete many work tasks faster and more easily.

It can help us understand information, create new content, and improve existing work.

For example, AI can help you:

- Summarize long reports.
- Understand complex information.
- Create emails and presentations.
- Generate images and ideas.
- Improve written content.
- Organize tasks and information.

AI is useful at work when we know how to give it clear instructions.

## 2. What Can AI Do?

There are three common ways to use AI at work.

### A. Organize and Understand Information

AI can help you understand large amounts of information quickly.

For example:
- Summarize long documents.
- Explain complex topics.
- Find important points in reports.
- Organize information from spreadsheets.
- Create simple lists from large amounts of data.

**Cybersecurity Example:**

Imagine you have a long security incident report.

You can ask Gemini:

```text
Read this security incident report.

Summarize:
1. What happened?
2. What systems were affected?
3. What are the main security risks?
4. What actions should be taken next?

Use simple English and bullet points.
```

This can help you understand the report faster. However, you must check the summary against the original report.

### B. Create New Content

AI can help you create new content from your ideas and instructions.

For example:
- Write emails.
- Create presentation outlines.
- Generate images.
- Brainstorm new ideas.
- Draft reports.
- Create study materials.

**Cybersecurity Example:**

Imagine you need to prepare a presentation about phishing attacks for university students.

You can ask Gemini:

```text
Create a presentation outline about phishing attacks.

Audience: University students.

Include:
1. What is phishing?
2. Common types of phishing.
3. Real-world examples.
4. Warning signs.
5. Prevention tips.

Use simple English and practical examples.
```

AI can help you prepare the first draft of your presentation.

### C. Improve Existing Work

AI can also help you improve content that you have already created.

For example:
- Correct grammar.
- Improve sentence structure.
- Make writing easier to understand.
- Suggest better ideas.
- Improve reports and presentations.

**Cybersecurity Example:**

Imagine you have written a basic report about a vulnerability.

You can ask Gemini:

```text
Review my vulnerability report.

Check:
1. Grammar and spelling.
2. Clarity of the explanation.
3. Missing important details.
4. Suggested improvements.

Keep the technical meaning unchanged.
```

AI can suggest improvements, but you should verify all technical details.

## 3. How to Use AI Effectively

The quality of AI output often depends on the instructions you provide.

These instructions are called **prompts**.

A prompt tells AI what you want it to do.

AI understands natural language, so you can communicate with it using everyday words.

### Example: Weak Prompt vs. Strong Prompt

**Weak Prompt:**

```text
Explain cybersecurity.
```

This prompt is too general.

**Strong Prompt:**

```text
Explain cybersecurity to a beginner.

Cover:
1. What cybersecurity means.
2. Why it is important.
3. Common cyber threats.
4. Basic protection methods.

Use simple English and real-life examples.
```

The second prompt gives AI more information about the task and the expected output.

### Tips for Writing Better Prompts

- Be clear about your goal.
- Give enough background information.
- Mention your target audience.
- Explain the format you want.
- Add important details and limitations.
- Use follow-up prompts to improve the answer.

## 4. Understand AI Models and AI Apps

An AI model and an AI app are connected, but they are not the same thing.

Think of a car:

- **Engine:** The engine provides the power.
- **Car:** The car gives you a way to use that power.
- **Controls:** The steering wheel and pedals help you control the car.

AI works in a similar way.

| Car Example | AI Example |
|---|---|
| Engine | AI Model |
| Car | AI App |
| Steering wheel and pedals | Chat box and app features |
| Driving instructions | Prompts |
| Car's result | AI Output |

### AI Model

An AI model is the technology that processes information and generates results.

For example, Gemini models power many AI features.

### AI App

An AI app is the tool or interface that people use to interact with an AI model.

For example, the Gemini app allows users to enter prompts and receive answers.

**Remember:** You usually interact with an AI model through an app instead of working with the model directly.

## 5. Different Models Have Different Strengths

Not all AI models work equally well for every task.

Some models may be better at:
- Writing and summarizing.
- Solving complex problems.
- Understanding images.
- Writing and explaining code.
- Analyzing large amounts of information.

Choose a model based on the task you want to complete.

## 6. Understand Temperature in AI

Temperature is a setting that can affect how an AI model generates responses.

It controls the level of variation or randomness in the model's output.

- **Low temperature:** Responses are usually more focused and predictable.
- **High temperature:** Responses may be more varied and creative.

**Example:**

Imagine you ask AI to write about cybersecurity awareness.

- With low temperature, it may give you a direct and simple explanation.
- With high temperature, it may suggest more creative ideas and different ways to present the topic.

**When to Use Each:**

| Temperature | Useful For |
|---|---|
| Low | Technical explanations, structured reports, factual tasks |
| High | Brainstorming, creative writing, new ideas |

*Note: Temperature settings may not be available in every AI app or model.*

## 7. Practical Cybersecurity Workflow

Imagine you are studying a security incident report in a cybersecurity lab.

You can use AI in these steps:

1. **Provide input:** Add the report or relevant security logs.
2. **Give a prompt:** Ask AI to summarize the incident.
3. **Review the output:** Read the findings and suggested actions.
4. **Verify the details:** Check the original evidence and confirm the technical information.
5. **Improve the report:** Ask AI to make the explanation clearer.
6. **Make decisions:** Use your own judgment to decide what actions are appropriate.

Never upload passwords, private logs, personal information, or confidential company data to an AI tool unless you are authorized and the tool is approved for that data.

## 8. Key Notes

- AI can organize information, create content, and improve existing work.
- Prompts are instructions that guide AI.
- Clear and specific prompts can produce more useful results.
- AI apps provide an interface to interact with AI models.
- Different AI models have different strengths.
- Temperature can affect the creativity and variation of AI responses.
- Always review and verify AI-generated information.
- Do not share sensitive information with unapproved AI tools.
