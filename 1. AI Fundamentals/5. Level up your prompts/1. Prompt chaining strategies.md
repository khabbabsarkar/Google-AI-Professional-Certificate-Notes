## Introduction

Prompt chaining is a technique that uses multiple connected prompts to complete a large task.

Instead of asking AI to complete everything at once, we divide the work into smaller steps.

In this lab, we will practice prompt chaining in Gemini using a business example.

**Tip:** Sign in to Gemini with your Google Account. This gives you access to additional features and allows you to save your Gemini activity.

## Step 1: Brainstorm Options

First, open Gemini and click **New Chat**.

Ask Gemini to suggest new services for a business.

### Example Prompt

> I want to expand my cybersecurity training business's service offerings. Act as my business advisor and list three complementary services we could provide.
>
> Keep in mind:
> - We have a small team of three people.
> - We have a limited budget.
> - We want to provide online services.
> - Our target audience is beginner cybersecurity students.

Gemini will suggest three possible services.

Read the suggestions carefully. You will use them in the next step.

## Step 2: Evaluate the Options

Now, use the same chat.

Ask Gemini to evaluate its previous suggestions and identify the best options.

### Example Prompt

> Which of these services can we launch within six months? Compare them based on cost, required skills, demand, and difficulty.

Gemini will compare the options and explain their possible advantages and challenges.

You can also ask follow-up questions to understand the suggestions better.

## Step 3: Create an Action Plan

After choosing one service, ask Gemini to create a practical action plan.

### Example Prompt

> If I want to move forward with the beginner cybersecurity training service, what tasks should I complete this week? Create a simple action plan with tasks, priorities, and expected results.

Gemini will provide a list of tasks to help you get started.

Review the plan and use it to organize your weekly activities.

## 4. Cybersecurity Learning Example

Prompt chaining can also help you plan your own cybersecurity learning journey.

### Prompt 1: Identify Skills

> Act as a cybersecurity career advisor. I am a student interested in penetration testing, digital forensics, SOC operations, and AI security. Suggest three skills I should focus on to prepare for an entry-level cybersecurity role.

### Prompt 2: Evaluate Skills

> Compare these three skills based on job opportunities, beginner difficulty, practical learning requirements, and free learning resources. Which one should I learn first?

### Prompt 3: Create an Action Plan

> I want to start learning [selected skill]. Create a seven-day study plan with daily topics, practical activities, and free learning resources.

### Prompt 4: Track Progress

> Create a simple progress tracker for this seven-day learning plan. Include the day, topic, practical task, and completion status.

## 5. Prompt Chaining Workflow

<pre>
Step 1: Brainstorm Options
          ↓
Step 2: Evaluate Options
          ↓
Step 3: Select the Best Option
          ↓
Step 4: Create an Action Plan
          ↓
Step 5: Review and Track Progress
</pre>

Each step uses information from the previous step.

## Key Notes

- Start with a clear goal.
- Break large tasks into smaller steps.
- Use the same chat to maintain context.
- Build each new prompt on the previous response.
- Evaluate AI suggestions before making decisions.
- Turn the final result into practical actions.
- Review AI-generated plans before following them.
