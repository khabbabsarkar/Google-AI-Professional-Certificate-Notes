## 1. Introduction

Gemini is an AI assistant that can help us generate ideas, plan presentations, and improve our work.

In this lab, we learn how to use Gemini for brainstorming.

Brainstorming means creating and exploring different ideas before choosing the best one.

**Before starting:**
- Sign in to Gemini using your Google Account.
- This helps you use more features.
- It also allows you to save your Gemini activity.
- Start a new chat before following the lab steps.

## 2. Lab Tasks

### Step 1: Brainstorm Presentation Structures

In this step, we use Gemini to generate different ways to organize a presentation.

**How to do it:**

1. Open Gemini.
2. Click the **New Chat** button.
3. Copy the prompt below.
4. Replace the information in brackets with your own details.
5. Send the prompt to Gemini.

**Prompt:**

```text
I'm preparing a presentation on [Cybersecurity Awareness].

My main goal is to [explain common cyber threats and teach people how to stay safe online].

Help me brainstorm 5 interesting ways to structure my presentation for an audience of [university students and beginners in cybersecurity].

Include a clear introduction, main topics, practical examples, and a conclusion.
```

**Expected result:**

Gemini will suggest different presentation structures. You can review them and choose the one that best matches your goal.

### Step 2: Find a Hook Statistic

A hook is an interesting fact, question, or statistic that catches the audience's attention at the beginning of a presentation.

In this step, we ask Gemini to find a surprising statistic related to our topic.

**How to do it:**

1. Continue using the same Gemini chat.
2. Choose your favorite presentation idea from Step 1.
3. Copy the prompt below.
4. Replace the information in brackets.
5. Send it to Gemini.

**Prompt:**

```text
I like the presentation idea about [Cybersecurity Awareness].

Now I need a powerful hook for my introduction.

Find a surprising and recent statistic about [cybercrime, phishing attacks, or data breaches].

Use a reliable source and include the source name, publication date, and link.

Explain why this statistic is important for my audience.
```

**Expected result:**

Gemini will suggest statistics that can make your presentation more interesting.

**Important:** Always check statistics and their original sources before using them in your presentation.

### Step 3: Draft Your First Paragraph

In this step, we use Gemini to write the opening paragraph of our presentation.

**How to do it:**

1. Continue using the same Gemini chat.
2. Choose your favorite hook from Step 2.
3. Copy the prompt below.
4. Replace the information in brackets.
5. Send it to Gemini.

**Prompt:**

```text
I like the statistic about [your selected cybersecurity statistic].

Using this statistic, write an opening paragraph for my presentation on [Cybersecurity Awareness].

Generate 3 different versions with different tones:

1. Casual and friendly.
2. Professional and informative.
3. Powerful and attention-grabbing.

Use simple English, keep the audience interested, and make the introduction suitable for university students and cybersecurity beginners.
```

**Expected result:**

Gemini will create three opening paragraphs with different tones. You can select your favorite version and improve it.

## 3. Other Ways to Use Gemini for Cybersecurity

You can also use Gemini to brainstorm ideas for different cybersecurity activities.

**Examples:**

- **Penetration Testing:** Generate ideas for testing web application security in an authorized lab.
- **Digital Forensics:** Brainstorm steps for investigating a digital incident.
- **SOC Operations:** Generate ideas for improving security alert investigation.
- **Network Security:** Explore ways to improve network protection.
- **AI Security:** Brainstorm possible risks of prompt injection and other LLM attacks.
- **Security Awareness:** Create ideas for phishing awareness campaigns.
- **Incident Response:** Develop different scenarios for cybersecurity incident response exercises.
