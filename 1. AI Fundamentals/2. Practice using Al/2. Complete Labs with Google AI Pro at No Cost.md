## 1. Introduction

In this certificate, we will not only learn about AI but also build projects using AI tools.

Google AI experts and experienced professionals will guide us through the course. They will teach us how to use AI tools in real-life work.

To get more from the practical labs, we can use professional AI tools.

## 2. Get 3 Months of Google AI Pro for Free

Google provides a **free 3-month trial of Google AI Pro** for eligible learners in this certificate.

This trial gives us higher access to advanced AI models and features.

**Benefits include:**
- Access to more powerful AI models.
- Use of advanced AI features.
- Better support for hands-on labs.
- More opportunities to build AI projects.
- Help in creating a strong project portfolio.

**Important:**
- The subscription is optional.
- It is not required to complete the labs.
- It is not required to earn the certificate.
- It is recommended for a better learning experience.

## 3. Eligibility Requirements

There are some conditions for using the free trial.

- **Country:** The offer is available only in countries where Google AI Pro is supported.
- **Age:** Users must meet Google's age requirements.
- **One-Time Use:** The offer can only be redeemed once.
- **Personal Account:** You need a personal Google account to redeem the offer.

## 4. Important Tips and Reminders

### A. Google Account Requirement

The offer is designed for personal Google accounts.

If you are using a Google Workspace account, switch to your personal Google account before redeeming the offer.

### B. Using AI at Work

If you use AI tools for work-related tasks, always follow your organization's AI policies and rules.

Do not share confidential company information without permission.

### C. Using AI at School or University

Before using AI tools for your labs or academic work:

- Check your institution's AI policy.
- Follow data privacy rules.
- Follow relevant compliance requirements, such as FERPA.
- Do not upload private student records or university data unless the AI tool is approved by your institution.

## 6. Is Google AI Pro Required for This Certificate?

No. A Google AI Pro subscription is not required to complete the certificate or earn the certificate.

However, it is recommended because it provides higher access to advanced AI models and features, which can improve the hands-on learning experience.
