## 1. Introduction

To learn AI, we need to practice using AI tools.

In this certificate, we will complete **20+ hands-on labs**. These labs are based on real tasks that people do at work.

Each lab includes:
- An expert-led video tutorial.
- Step-by-step written instructions.
- Practical activities using AI tools.

By completing these labs, we can build a portfolio of AI projects and use our new skills at work.

**Important:** You must use a desktop or laptop computer to access the labs. Mobile phones and tablets are not supported.

## 2. How to Launch a Lab

Follow these steps to start a lab:

1. Open the lab using a desktop or laptop.
2. Click the **Launch Lab** button.
3. A new browser tab will open.
4. Choose one of the two learning options.

There are two ways to complete a lab.

### Option 1: Seamless Login

This option opens the AI tool in a new browser tab.

- One tab contains the AI tool for practice.
- Another tab contains the video tutorial and written instructions.
- You can switch between the tabs while learning.

### Option 2: Side-by-Side Learning

<badge color="success">Recommended option</badge>

This option opens a split-screen workspace.

- **Left Side:** The AI tool, such as Gemini, where you can practice.
- **Right Side:** The video tutorial and written lab instructions.

This makes it easier to follow the instructions while practicing with AI.

You can choose either option for each lab.

**Important:** Sign in to your Google account before using Gemini so you can save your work.

## 3. How to Complete a Lab Using Side-by-Side Learning

When you select side-by-side learning, your screen will be divided into two parts.

### Left Side: AI Practice Area

- Gemini will open in a virtual browser.
- You can practice using AI tools.
- Sign in to your Google account to save your work.
- If you want to use another AI tool, enter its website address in the browser's address bar.

### Right Side: Learning Instructions

You will find:
- A video tutorial from a Google AI instructor.
- Step-by-step written instructions.
- Sample prompts to use during the lab.

**Note:** AI tools like Gemini are updated regularly. Your screen may look a little different from the video tutorial. However, you can still complete the lab by following the written instructions.

## 4. How to Use Sample Prompts

The written instructions include sample prompts highlighted in grey.

Follow these steps to use them:

1. **Copy the Prompt:** Select the sample prompt, right-click, and choose Copy.
2. **Paste the Prompt:** Go to Gemini, right-click in the prompt box, and select Paste.
3. **Edit the Prompt:** Replace the text inside the brackets with your own information.
4. **Submit the Prompt:** Send it to Gemini and review the response.

**Important:** Keyboard shortcuts for copying and pasting may not work in Coursera labs. Use the right-click options instead.

## 5. How to Complete a Lab Using Seamless Login

If you select seamless login:

1. Two browser windows or tabs will open.
2. One will show the AI tool.
3. The other will show the video tutorial and written instructions.
4. Keep both open while completing the lab.
5. Follow the instructions and practice with the AI tool.

## 6. How to Use Files and Documents in Labs

Some labs require you to upload files, such as reports or spreadsheets.

You can use your own files or the sample files provided in the course.

### Option 1: Use Sample Files

1. Click the file icon at the top of the lab instructions.
2. Click **Use Template** in the top-right corner.
3. The file will open and be saved to your Google Drive.
4. You can use the file from Google Drive or upload it to the AI tool.

### Option 2: Upload Your Own Files

1. Open the AI tool.
2. Click the **+** icon in the prompt box.
3. Select **Add from Drive**.
4. Choose the file you want to upload.

## 7. How to Finish a Lab

After completing all the lab activities:

1. Scroll to the end of the written instructions.
2. Click **Mark as Completed**.
3. Close the lab browser tab.
4. Return to the Launch Lab page.
5. Click **Go to Next Item** to continue the course.

## 8. Apply What You Learn

Don't just complete the labs to finish the course.

Try to use your new AI skills in your daily life, studies, and professional work.

For example:
- Use AI to improve your writing.
- Analyze data with AI.
- Create presentations.
- Solve real-world problems.
- Build useful AI-powered projects.

The best way to learn AI is through hands-on practice.

This certificate provides 20+ practical labs where you can learn, experiment, and build real AI projects.
