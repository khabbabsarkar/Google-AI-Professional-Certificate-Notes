## 1. What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is becoming an important part of our daily life and work. It works like a smart assistant that helps us complete tasks faster and more easily.

**Examples of AI in daily life:**
- Finding the fastest route to work.
- Suggesting videos based on our interests.
- Creating presentations and charts.
- Helping us solve problems and understand new topics.

## 2. Purpose of the Course

The main goal of this course is to teach us how to use AI tools in our daily work and professional life.

**We will learn how to:**
- Understand the basics of AI.
- Use AI tools to complete tasks.
- Improve our work and productivity.
- Use AI in a safe and responsible way.

## 3. Hands-on Learning

This course focuses on practical learning, not just theory.

We will practice using AI tools such as:

- **Gemini App:** An AI assistant that helps with different tasks.
- **Google AI Studio:** A tool for exploring and building AI-powered projects.

## 4. What Will We Learn?

After completing this course, we will be able to:

- Ask AI questions and get useful answers.
- Learn about new topics quickly.
- Create presentations and marketing materials.
- Create charts and data visualizations.
- Build simple AI-powered applications without coding.
- Solve real-world problems using AI.

## 5. Certificate and Career Benefits

After completing the course, learners will receive a Google certificate.

**This course can help us:**
- Improve our AI skills.
- Increase work productivity.
- Build a portfolio of AI projects.
- Show our skills to employers.
- Find better career opportunities.

AI is not only for technical experts. Anyone can use AI tools to make daily tasks easier, improve productivity, solve problems, and develop their career skills.

**The best way to learn AI is through practice.**
