## 1. Introduction

AI tools like Gemini can sometimes give answers that are incorrect, incomplete, or not useful.

When this happens, you should not simply accept the answer. You can give feedback to help improve the response.

Giving feedback is an important part of responsible AI use.

## 2. What Should You Do If You Get a Bad Response?

If Gemini gives you a bad or unhelpful response, you can use the **Thumbs Down (👎)** button.

This tells Gemini that the response did not meet your needs.

Sometimes, a feedback box will appear. It may ask you to explain what went wrong.

You can:

- Select one of the available feedback options.
- Write your own explanation.
- Explain what you expected from the answer.
- Describe how the response could be improved.

## 3. How to Give Useful Feedback

Good feedback should explain both the problem and your expectation.

### Example 1: Incorrect Information

**Problem:** Gemini gives an incorrect explanation of a cybersecurity topic.

**Feedback:**

```text
The response contains incorrect technical information.
Please provide an accurate explanation and use
reliable cybersecurity sources.
```

### Example 2: Not Enough Detail

**Problem:** Gemini gives a very short answer when you need a detailed explanation.

**Feedback:**

```text
The answer is too short.
I expected a more detailed explanation with
simple examples and practical use cases.
```

### Example 3: Outdated Information

**Problem:** Gemini suggests an outdated cybersecurity practice.

**Feedback:**

```text
The suggested information may be outdated.
Please use current security practices and
mention reliable sources for verification.
```

## 4. Real-Life Cybersecurity Example

### Scenario: AI-Powered Security Incident Response

Imagine a cybersecurity team uses an AI tool to analyze security incidents and suggest solutions.

Sometimes, the AI may:

- Miss an important vulnerability.
- Suggest an outdated security solution.
- Ignore an important risk.
- Give an incomplete incident response plan.

### How to Give Feedback

**Step 1: Identify the problem**

Review the AI response and find what is incorrect or missing.

**Step 2: Give feedback**

Use the thumbs-down option and explain the problem.

**Step 3: Explain your expectation**

Tell the developers what a better response should include.

**Example feedback:**

```text
The suggested mitigation steps are incomplete.
The response does not consider the risk of
unauthorized access.

I expected a more detailed response that includes
access control, evidence collection, and incident
containment steps.
```

**Step 4: Verify the next response**

If you ask Gemini again, check its answer carefully. Do not assume that feedback guarantees an immediate correction.

## 5. Why Is User Feedback Important?

User feedback helps AI developers understand where their models struggle.

Feedback can help developers:

- Identify incorrect or unhelpful responses.
- Understand common user problems.
- Find areas where the model needs improvement.
- Improve the quality and usefulness of future responses.

**Important:** Feedback may be reviewed and used to improve AI systems. Do not include passwords, private information, confidential data, or sensitive security details in feedback.

## 6. Best Practices for Responsible AI Use

1. **Do not blindly trust AI:** Always review important answers.
2. **Give clear feedback:** Explain exactly what went wrong.
3. **Describe your expectations:** Tell the AI developers what would make the answer more useful.
4. **Verify important information:** Use trusted sources to check facts.
5. **Protect private data:** Never share sensitive information unnecessarily.
6. **Keep human control:** Make important decisions using human judgment.

## 7. Quick Summary

| Topic | Key Point |
|---|---|
| Thumbs Down | Shows that a response was not helpful. |
| Feedback Box | Allows you to explain the problem. |
| Useful Feedback | Describes what was wrong and what you expected. |
| Feedback Analysis | Helps developers identify model weaknesses. |
| Responsible Use | Review answers and protect sensitive data. |

## Key Notes

- AI responses are not always correct or useful.
- Use the thumbs-down button when a response is unsatisfactory.
- Explain the problem clearly.
- Tell developers what you expected from the response.
- User feedback can help improve AI systems over time.
- Always verify important information before using it.
