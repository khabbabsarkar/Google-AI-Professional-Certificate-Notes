## Introduction

Gemini can help you choose a new skill and create a learning plan.

In this lab, we use a step-by-step prompting framework to get better and more personal results.

**Tip:** Sign in to Gemini with your Google Account. This gives you access to more features and lets you save your Gemini activity.

## Step 1: Define Your Initial Task

First, open Gemini and start a **New Chat**.

Use this prompt to explain your main goal:

**Example Prompt:**

> I want to learn a new skill to advance my career in cybersecurity, but I'm not sure where to start. Help me brainstorm some ideas.

Gemini will suggest different skills based on your goal.

Read the suggestions. You will improve them in the next steps.

## Step 2: Use Meta-Prompting to Improve the Results

Meta-prompting means asking AI how to improve its own answers.

Instead of accepting the first suggestions, ask Gemini what information it needs.

**Example Prompt:**

> What questions can I answer to help you tailor your suggestions?

Gemini will ask questions about your interests, goals, experience, and available time.

These questions help Gemini understand your needs better.

## Step 3: Add Context About Yourself

Now, answer Gemini's questions.

Give it useful information about your background, interests, and goals.

**Example Prompt:**

> Here are some more details about me:
>
> - I am a student interested in cybersecurity and information security.
> - I have basic knowledge of networking, Linux, and cybersecurity.
> - I am interested in penetration testing, digital forensics, SOC operations, and AI security.
> - I can spend a few hours each week learning new skills.
>
> Based on this information, suggest three skills that would be relevant to me.

Gemini can now give suggestions based on your personal context instead of general advice.

## Step 4: Define a Persona

A persona tells Gemini what role it should act as.

This can help Gemini give advice from a specific point of view.

**Example Prompt:**

> Now, act as an experienced cybersecurity career coach. Which of the skills above would you recommend I focus on first? Explain your reasoning.

Gemini will review the suggested skills from a career coach's point of view.

## Step 5: Decide on Next Steps

After choosing a skill, ask Gemini to create a simple action plan.

**Example Prompt:**

> Suggest three actions I can take this week to start learning that skill. Format this as a table with three columns: Action, Learning Resource, and Expected Result.

Gemini will create a simple plan to help you get started.

## Key Prompting Techniques Used

| Technique | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Initial Task | Explain what you want to achieve. |
| Meta-Prompting | Ask AI how to improve its suggestions. |
| Context | Share relevant details about yourself. |
| Persona | Give AI a specific role to follow. |
| Output Format | Tell AI how to organize the answer. |

## Key Notes

- Start with a clear goal.
- Do not accept the first AI response without reviewing it.
- Use meta-prompting to improve the results.
- Give Gemini enough context to understand your needs.
- Use personas to get advice from different points of view.
- Ask for clear next steps instead of general suggestions.
- Review AI-generated advice and check important information.
