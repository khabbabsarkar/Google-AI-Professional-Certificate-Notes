# Effective Prompting for AI

## 1. What Is Prompting?

Prompting means giving instructions or questions to an AI tool to get the result you want.

A prompt is simply the message you send to AI.

Think about ordering a sandwich.

If you only say "I want a sandwich," you may get something you do not like.

But if you say "I want a turkey sandwich with lettuce and tomato," your request is much clearer.

AI works in a similar way.

**The clearer your prompt, the better AI can understand your needs.**

## 2. The Four Main Parts of a Good Prompt

A good prompt often includes four important parts:

### A. Persona

Tell AI what role or expertise you want it to use.

For example:
- Act as a cybersecurity expert.
- Act as an English teacher.
- Act as an experienced project manager.
- Act as a network security trainer.

### B. Task

Clearly explain what you want AI to do.

For example:
- Explain a topic.
- Summarize a report.
- Create a study plan.
- Find weaknesses in a sample security report.

### C. Format

Tell AI how you want the answer to look.

For example:
- Bullet points.
- A table.
- A step-by-step guide.
- A short paragraph.
- A presentation outline.

### D. Context

Give AI useful background information about your situation.

For example:
- I am a beginner in cybersecurity.
- I am preparing for a university presentation.
- My audience has no technical knowledge.
- I am studying network security in a practice lab.

## 3. Example: A Weak Prompt vs. A Strong Prompt

**Weak Prompt:**

```text
Explain phishing.
```

This prompt does not explain your level, purpose, or preferred format.

**Strong Prompt:**

```text
Persona: Act as a cybersecurity trainer.

Task: Explain phishing attacks.

Context: I am a beginner in cybersecurity.
I want to understand how phishing works and how to identify it.

Format:
1. Simple definition.
2. How phishing works.
3. Three real-life examples.
4. Warning signs.
5. Prevention tips.

Use easy English and short bullet points.
```

The strong prompt gives AI clear instructions and useful context.

## 4. Use Iterative Prompting

You do not need to get the perfect answer with your first prompt.

**Iterative prompting** means improving an AI response by asking follow-up questions or giving more instructions.

For example:

**Step 1: Ask for an explanation.**

```text
Explain how a phishing attack works.
Use simple English.
```

**Step 2: Ask for more details.**

```text
Explain the five common stages of a phishing attack.
Give a simple example for each stage.
```

**Step 3: Ask for practical advice.**

```text
Now explain how a beginner can identify
and prevent these phishing attacks.
```

**Step 4: Ask for a different format.**

```text
Turn the explanation into a short
cybersecurity awareness presentation outline.
```

This step-by-step process helps you improve the result.

## 5. More Tips for Effective Prompting

### A. Give Examples

Examples help AI understand what kind of output you expect.

For example:

```text
Write a LinkedIn post about phishing awareness.

Use this style:
- A strong opening line.
- Simple English.
- A practical cybersecurity lesson.
- A question at the end.

Avoid robotic and overly formal language.
```

### B. Use Images and Documents

Many AI tools support more than text.

You may be able to provide:
- Images.
- PDF files.
- Documents.
- Spreadsheets.
- Screenshots.

**Example:**

Upload a screenshot of a network diagram and ask:

```text
Explain this network diagram.

Identify:
1. Main network devices.
2. How the devices connect.
3. The possible security risks.

Explain everything in simple English.
```

Always check whether the AI has correctly understood the uploaded content.

### C. Add Constraints

Constraints are rules or limits that guide AI's response.

For example:

```text
Explain SQL injection.

Rules:
- Use simple English.
- Keep the answer under 300 words.
- Include one safe example.
- Do not include advanced technical details.
```

### D. Keep Chats Focused

Try to use separate conversations for different tasks.

For example:
- One chat for learning Python.
- One chat for cybersecurity notes.
- One chat for LinkedIn content.
- One chat for penetration testing concepts.

This helps keep the conversation organized and reduces confusion.

## 6. Practical Cybersecurity Example: Improving Incident Response Meetings

Imagine you are preparing an incident response meeting in a cybersecurity training scenario.

The meeting includes different teams, and they need to understand the incident, make decisions, and assign tasks quickly.

You can use the four-part prompting framework.

**Example Prompt:**

```text
Persona:
Act as an experienced cybersecurity incident response leader.

Task:
Suggest ways to make incident response meetings
more effective.

Context:
The meeting includes security analysts, IT staff,
and management. The teams need to make quick
decisions during a security incident.

Format:
Provide a bullet list with practical suggestions.

Constraints:
- Focus on actions that can be started within 24 hours.
- Avoid solutions that require buying new software.
- Use simple and professional English.

Also include a short meeting agenda
and a list of important action items.
```

AI may suggest:
- Assigning clear roles to team members.
- Setting priorities for urgent tasks.
- Keeping meetings focused on important issues.
- Recording decisions and action items.
- Setting deadlines for each task.

You can then use follow-up prompts to improve the suggestions.

*Note: This is a hypothetical example. AI can support planning, but real incident response decisions must be based on verified evidence and authorized procedures.*

## 7. A Simple Prompting Formula

You can use this formula for many tasks:

**Persona + Task + Context + Format + Constraints**

| Part | Question to Ask |
|---|---|
| Persona | Who should AI act as? |
| Task | What should AI do? |
| Context | What background does AI need? |
| Format | How should the answer look? |
| Constraints | What rules should AI follow? |

You do not need to include every part in every prompt. Use the parts that are useful for your task.

## 8. Key Notes

- Prompting means giving instructions to AI.
- Clear and detailed prompts help AI understand your needs.
- Persona tells AI what role to take.
- Task explains what you want AI to do.
- Context gives AI background information.
- Format controls how the answer should look.
- Iterative prompting helps improve results step by step.
- Examples, images, and documents can provide extra context.
- Constraints help control the output.
- Always review AI-generated answers before using them.
