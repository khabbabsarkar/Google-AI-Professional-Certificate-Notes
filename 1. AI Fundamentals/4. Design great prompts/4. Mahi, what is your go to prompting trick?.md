## Introduction

To get better results from AI, do not just give it a short instruction and wait for the final answer.

Give AI enough background information about your task. Also, ask it to check whether it needs more details before starting the work.

This helps AI understand your needs and produce more useful results.

## 1. Provide Clear Context

Context means giving AI the information it needs to understand your task.

Useful context can include:

- Your main goal
- Background information
- Your requirements
- Your audience
- Available tools or resources
- Any limits or rules it must follow

**Example:**

Weak prompt:

> Help me create a cybersecurity study plan.

Better prompt:

> I am a cybersecurity student with basic knowledge of networking and Linux. I want to improve my penetration testing skills. I can study for two hours each day. Create a beginner-friendly study plan using free learning resources.

The second prompt gives AI more information, so it can provide a more relevant answer.

## 2. Ask AI to Clarify Before Starting

Sometimes, your instructions may not contain enough information.

Instead of allowing AI to make assumptions, ask it to identify missing details and ask questions before starting.

**Useful Prompt:**

> Before starting this task, review my instructions. If you need more information or have any questions, ask me first. Do not start the task until you have enough details.

This allows AI to pause and ask questions when something is unclear.

## 3. Use an Interactive Feedback Process

Working with AI should be a conversation, not just a single request.

A simple process:

1. Explain your goal.
2. Provide background information.
3. Share your requirements and limitations.
4. Ask AI to identify missing details.
5. Answer its questions.
6. Let AI complete the task.
7. Review the result and give feedback.

This process can reduce unnecessary changes and improve the final result.

## 4. Real-Life Cybersecurity Example

### Incident Response Planning

Imagine a cybersecurity team needs to create an incident response plan for an organization.

Before starting, the manager provides important details:

- Network architecture
- Common security threats
- Available security tools
- Team roles and responsibilities
- Relevant security policies

The manager also says:

> Before creating the incident response plan, review all the information. If anything is missing or unclear, ask me questions first. Wait for my answers before preparing the final plan.

The team can now clarify important details, such as who will investigate an incident and which tools are available.

**Why is this important?**

- Reduces misunderstandings.
- Helps avoid incorrect assumptions.
- Makes the plan more useful.
- Reduces unnecessary revisions.
- Improves communication during security work.

This is a hypothetical example of how the same communication method can be used in cybersecurity.

## 5. Reusable Prompt Template

You can use this prompt with Gemini or other AI tools:

> **My Goal:** [Explain what you want to achieve.]
>
> **Background:** [Provide important information about the task.]
>
> **Requirements:** [Explain what you need.]
>
> **Limitations:** [Mention any rules, tools, time, or other limits.]
>
> **Expected Output:** [Explain what the final result should look like.]
>
> Before starting, carefully review all the information. If you need more details or have questions, ask me first. Do not begin the task until the important details are clear.

## Key Notes

- Give AI clear and useful background information.
- Explain your goal and requirements.
- Do not expect AI to understand missing details.
- Encourage AI to ask questions before starting.
- Review the output and provide feedback.
- Treat AI as a collaborator, not just an answer generator.
